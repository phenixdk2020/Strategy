## Higher Command HQ — Division, Brigade, Regiment, Battalion

Status: **DESIGN COMMITTED / IMPLEMENTATION NEXT**.

F30B introduces the higher-command structure now, before artillery is added, so cavalry and artillery are not forced into the infantry battalion/regiment hierarchy.

## Command chain

The tactical command hierarchy is:

`DIVISIONSCHEF -> BRIGADECHEF -> OBERST/OBERSTLØJTNANT -> MAJOR -> KAPTAJN`

Roles are command appointments. Historical officer rank/name remains scenario/OOB data and must not be hard-coded as identical to the command role.

## Organic parent vs attachment

Every tactical unit/HQ can distinguish:

- `OrganicParent`: its permanent OOB parent;
- `CurrentCommandParent`: the HQ currently exercising tactical command;
- `AttachmentType`: Organic / Attached / Detached / Reserve.

Infantry companies remain organic to their battalion and regiment.

Cavalry and artillery are independent arms and must not be modelled as extra infantry companies under a Major. They can be held by Brigade/Division and temporarily attached downward for a mission.

## HQ responsibilities

### Divisionschef

- assigns brigade objectives;
- allocates reserve, cavalry and artillery between brigades;
- chooses main effort and broad defensive/attack sectors;
- should not micromanage companies.

### Brigadechef

- assigns missions to regiments;
- allocates attached cavalry/artillery to regimental tasks;
- coordinates combined-arms support;
- maintains brigade reserve.

### Regimental commander

- commands battalions and temporarily attached assets;
- translates brigade mission to battalion missions;
- does not replace local cavalry/artillery commander logic.

### Major

- commands organic infantry companies of one battalion;
- does not permanently own cavalry or artillery.

## UI/OOB rule

OOB must visibly represent the full chain and distinguish organic from attached assets. Selecting Division/Brigade HQ is normal command selection with the same single-click/double-click navigation semantics used by lower HQs.
