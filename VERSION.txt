v00.01.00c2 - Soldier Visual Upgrade + Hover Info
- Builds directly on v00.01.00c Gate A+B+C.
- Active force remains exactly eight independent Companies: four Danish and four Prussian.
- Active manpower remains Denmark 759 + Prussia 812 = 1571 men.
- Company OOB identity, UnitID, world pose and selection ownership remain unchanged.
- Permanent world-space TextMesh Company labels are removed.
- Mouse-over information panel now shows Regiment, Battalion, Company, nation, strength, formation, UnitID and selected state.
- Selection behaviour remains unchanged: LMB select, Shift add, Ctrl toggle, drag-box select, Esc clear.
- Soldier silhouette upgraded from simple block figure to a more articulated low-poly infantryman.

- No national/regimental standards in this revision; flag work is deliberately deferred.
- Movement/navigation remains OFF until Gate D.
- Company combat remains OFF until Gate E.
- Officer AI, Brigade AI, artillery and cavalry remain inactive.
