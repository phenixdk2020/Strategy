Scenario: Danish test regiments are defenders; Prussian test regiments are attackers
QA OOB expansion: TEST now contains four Danish and four Prussian infantry regiments (eight total) arranged in four starting lanes per side for group-control and multi-unit AI testing
Attack frontage deconfliction: when several AI-controlled regiments converge on the same enemy, the first engager retains the primary frontage while later arrivals are assigned distinct lateral frontage slots beside it instead of converging on the target centre
Attack frontage V2: secondary frontage slots are scored against travel distance, blocked destinations, houses/trees/fences, river crossings, line-to-target obstruction and friendly separation so later arrivals prefer a genuinely usable side of the engagement instead of parking behind a building or another regiment
Attack frontage hold rule: a secondary regiment that reaches its assigned frontage slot holds that local position and faces the target instead of receiving a new centre-seeking OrderAttack that would recreate overlap
Attack spacing telemetry: AI-SPACING-V2 logs unit, target, slot and final obstacle-aware slot position when frontage allocation activates
Battlefield navigation TEST layer: farmhouse, barn, trees and fence posts are treated as blocked tactical space for regiment movement and destination placement
Navigation V4: local tangent steering is replaced by a persistent A* route over a 4-unit tactical grid; the original player/AI destination is preserved while V4 owns short-term steering points around trees, buildings, fences and the river
Navigation V4 obstacle rule: route planning uses a compact marching clearance and automatically changes a regiment to COLUMN when an intermediate A* route is required; the final destination is validated with LINE clearance so a deployed formation cannot finish on top of an obstacle
Navigation V4 recovery: if a regiment starts or becomes trapped inside blocked tactical space, it is moved to the nearest legal escape point and the original route is replanned instead of remaining stalled against a tree
Navigation V4 stuck recovery: a regiment that makes effectively no progress for roughly 0.85 simulation seconds automatically replans its route and performs an emergency escape if no legal A* route can initially be generated
Navigation v00.00.09a footprint hotfix: an additional post-path steering layer checks the actual broad LINE/COLUMN formation footprint rather than only the regiment centre point; a LINE that would touch a tree/building narrows to COLUMN before contact
Navigation v00.00.09a initial-overlap rule: if a QA regiment initially overlaps a tree/building footprint it is moved to the nearest legal formation position at startup instead of beginning trapped inside the obstacle
Navigation v00.00.09a anti-snag rule: if the compact marching footprint still approaches/touches an obstacle, deterministic lateral/outward avoidance is applied without replacing the original player/AI destination
Navigation v00.00.09a stuck rule: lack of progress near an obstacle triggers a safe emergency relocation around that obstacle while preserving the active route; the unit then continues toward the original target
Navigation v00.00.09a telemetry: NAV-09A reports startup adjustment, forced COLUMN, push-out avoidance, emergency relocation and formation restore events
Navigation V4 telemetry: NAV-V4 reports direct/A* route creation, route point counts, stuck replans, emergency unstick/escape and no-route failures
Navigation compatibility: V3 and the previous active navigation/recovery steering layers remain in the TEST repository for reference/static queries but are disabled once Navigation V4 takes control
River crossing rule: the stream is non-traversable terrain for infantry; A* movement can cross it only through the fixed bridge zone
Bridge march rule: bridge/obstacle routes use COLUMN-sized movement clearance; pontoon construction remains reserved for later engineer capability
Pontoon rule: no regiment may cross open water merely by moving through it; pontoon construction is reserved for a later engineer-attachment capability and is not available without engineer support
Melee TEST layer: opposing infantry formations that physically contact each other and are not separated by water/solid obstacles enter bayonet/melee contact instead of walking through each other or continuing point-blank volley fire
Melee resolution: contact holds both formations, faces them toward each other, applies repeated strength/morale/cohesion losses, preserves normal rout thresholds and feeds casualty visuals through strength loss
Melee telemetry: MELEE-DIAG announces contact and MELEE-LOG records both units, distance, losses, remaining strength, morale and cohesion per melee pulse
Battlefield: doubled from 180x120 to 360x240 with expanded camera bounds and greater starting separation
Fire geometry: directional 120-degree forward fire fan follows regiment facing; no 360-degree firing ring
Range bands: Close <= 50% EffectiveRange; Medium <= EffectiveRange; Long <= MaximumRange
Range fan visibility: Close/Medium arcs and outer Long fan are terrain-following, unlit, thicker and higher-contrast so no side disappears into uneven ground
Fire policy: HOLD / CLOSE / MEDIUM / LONG determines when a regiment may open fire
Accuracy: TEST tuning now compensates the legacy kernel so Close/Medium/Long multipliers are applied exactly once instead of stacking on top of the old distance curve
Combat tuning menu: F9 or KAMP F9 opens runtime sliders for base hit chance per firing man, Close/Medium/Long factors, firing fraction, morale/cohesion accuracy floors, starting ammunition rounds per man and losses per visible casualty body
Combat tuning defaults: base hit chance 1.40%, Close x1.75, Medium x1.00, Long x0.30, 58% firing fraction, morale floor 72%, cohesion floor 68%, 60 starting rounds per man and 8 losses per visible casualty body
Morale/cohesion accuracy revision: morale and cohesion still reduce hit probability, but accuracy no longer collapses toward zero merely because prolonged close-range fire has reduced both values while hundreds of men remain formed
Volley diagnostics: every detected volley writes VOLLEY-LOG to Unity Console with battle time, shooter, target, distance, range band, fire policy, strength, firing men, morale, cohesion, quality multiplier, base hit chance, range multiplier, expected hits, resolved hits, target strength and ammunition before/after
Fire eligibility MVP: target must be inside selected fire-policy range and inside the forward fire arc
Reload: weapon profile base reload + bounded regiment Experience modifier retained from v00.00.08
Volley feedback revision: TEST layer detects every resolved volley and shows temporary Ramte N including Ramte 0; feedback lifetime increased for readability
Ammunition TEST model: starting rounds per man are configurable; one volley consumes one round per man; display is rounds per man, not total cartridges; no automatic regeneration
Officer assignment: every prototype regiment receives an OfficerProfile + OfficerAIController at runtime
Officer core stats: Leadership, Inspiration, Tactical Skill, Initiative, Staff/Command Skill, Discipline/Obedience, Aggressiveness/Caution, Composure/Nerve
Officer background: Experience is separate and primarily reduces decision noise
Player delegation: I toggles AI UNIT ON/OFF for selected Danish regiment(s); A remains camera-left in WASD
Officer AI doctrine menu: DEFENSIVE / BALANCED / OFFENSIVE applies to selected Danish regiment(s)
Commander aggression intent: 0-100 slider biases cautious/aggressive execution, while officer Aggressiveness remains dominant (70% officer / 30% order before doctrine bias)
Fire discipline menu: HOLD / CLOSE / MEDIUM / LONG applies to selected Danish regiment(s)
