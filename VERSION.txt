  * Prussia: 8th Regiment (QA), approximately 2,460 men, 3 battalions, 12 companies.

- Active company tactical centres: 20 total (8 Danish + 12 Prussian).
- 11. Infanteri-Regiment and 18th Regiment remain defined/configured in the OOB data model but are removed from the active BattleManager roster and hidden in this QA scenario.

- Regimental HQ, dual standards, cavalry/artillery pilot assets and the underlying brigade/OOB code remain in the project.
- The retained 09k regiment-wide foot renderer is disabled after mounted Regimental HQ creation; 09l2 company renderer remains authoritative for ordinary infantry.
- PlayerCommander stale selection/routes for hidden regiments are removed before the hidden regiments are deactivated.
- Removed company tactical entities are deleted from the 09l2 company lists so the active company count is genuinely 20 rather than 40 hidden/visible mixed entities.

v00.00.09l2 - Company tactical control
- Every company has a lightweight tactical centre; ordinary soldiers remain GPU-instanced.
- Denmark: 2 battalions x 4 companies per regiment.
- Prussia: 3 battalions x 4 companies per regiment.
- LMB selects a Danish company; Shift adds; Ctrl toggles.
- LMB drag box-select selects company centres.
- RMB moves selected companies; Alt+RMB appends a waypoint; RMB drag sets final facing.
- F = Line, C = Column, H = Hold, Z/X rotate company facing.
- Double-clicking a company returns to whole-regiment selection.
- Manual company orders clear the parent regiment route, switch parent Officer AI OFF and set the parent regiment Hold to avoid competing movement writers.

v00.00.09l2 - Formation geometry
- 1:1 infantry is anchored to independent company tactical centres rather than one regiment-wide pivot.
- Company Line = 3 ranks, approximately 0.52 m file spacing / 0.76 m rank spacing.
- Company Column = 8 files across.
- Four companies form a battalion line; battalions occupy separate template rows.
- Company casualty slots are deterministically distributed so losses do not simply cut one end off a formation.
- Company movement still does not have independent V3 obstacle/path navigation; whole-regiment V3 remains the accepted steering path.
