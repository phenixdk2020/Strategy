v00.00.09f29l - Battlefield hardening
- Major HQ follows active missions independently of Major AI ON/OFF and targets roughly 105 m behind its battalion centre.
- Major follow point is refreshed after roughly 55 m of tactical movement so HQs do not lag far behind the fighting line.
- Oberstløjtnant follows active player/higher-command missions independently of Regimental AI ON/OFF and targets roughly 210 m behind the midpoint of both Majors.
- Regimental follow point is refreshed after roughly 90 m of tactical movement.
- Explicit manual HQ moves are preserved instead of being immediately overwritten; a new mission returns the HQ to normal follow behaviour.
- Withdrawal follow is placed on the safe rear side in the direction of withdrawal.
- Replaces the visually unreliable F29G water presentation with a denser continuous F29L river ribbon sampled against the actual rendered Battlefield Ground mesh.
- River width is 7.5 m, longitudinal sampling 1.25 m and water surface lift 0.24 m; older Stream renderers/F29G ribbon are hidden while navigation/colliders remain unchanged.
- Adds authoritative final visual Square positioning: displayed men physically interpolate from Line/Column into a hollow two-rank square over the existing formation timer.
- Square side length and collider scale with CurrentStrength, so casualties visibly produce a smaller square.
- Adds defensive arrival hysteresis: latch at 1.35 m, release at 3.20 m; F29E micro-correction is suppressed while latched so defending companies stop shuttling around the blue destination footprint.
- Under-fire reaction remains authoritative while a defensive slot is latched.
- Backup: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f29k-pre-09f29l-battlefield-hardening.

v00.00.09f29k - Charge right-click targeting
