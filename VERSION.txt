v00.00.09f29d - Semantic zoom + NATO tactical overlay
- Adds a new runtime semantic zoom manager with four visual levels: CLOSE, MEDIUM, OPERATIONAL and STRATEGIC.
- HQ visibility is prioritized: Major/Battalion and Regimental HQ receive fixed-screen-size tactical counters from moderate zoom, before generic company counters appear.
- Major/Battalion counters use NATO echelon II and Regimental/Oberstløjtnant uses III; company-scale infantry uses I.
- Operational zoom overlays all company-scale formations on both sides with NATO-style infantry counters, facing arrows, strength bars, current/initial men and morale; Danish counters also show ammunition.
- Friendly counters are blue, enemy red, selected units/HQ gold and routed units grey.
- Strategic zoom hides company/HQ MeshRenderers and ParticleSystemRenderers while leaving terrain, simulation, colliders, AI, LineRenderers, command links and order state active. The result is a 2D tactical-symbol layer over the 3D battlefield.
- Zooming back in restores the hidden 3D meshes.
- Dynamic refresh ensures newly created flag/HQ/company renderers inherit the current strategic visibility state.
- HQ counters include a screen-space beacon/stem down to the real world position so commanders remain locatable over terrain and units.
- The two company-scale Prussian QA entities are displayed in the semantic overlay as PR. 1. KOMPAGNI and PR. 2. KOMPAGNI, while their legacy internal identifiers remain unchanged for compatibility.
- Current QA thresholds use camera height: HQ markers ~72 m, Medium ~135 m, Operational ~235 m, Strategic ~390 m. A later design phase may replace this with projected screen-footprint thresholds.
- Adds a top-right tactical-view indicator while not in Close view.
- New telemetry prefix: SEMANTIC-ZOOM-09F29D.
- Design proposal and future path documented in docs/design-supplements/v00.00.09f29d-semantic-zoom-nato-symbols.md.
- Backup before this revision: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f29c-pre-09f29d-semantic-zoom.

v00.00.09f29c - Unified command HUD + company naming
- Adds one compact 90 px tactical command HUD for both Major/Battalion and Company/Kaptajn selection, visually replacing the overlapping legacy bottom controls without changing the existing simulation-control footprint.
- Major HUD is divided into ENHEDSINFO / AI, BATALJONSORDRER and KOMPAGNIER UNDER MAJOR.
- Major HUD shows aggregate men, losses, morale, cohesion and ammunition plus one status row for each of the four subordinate companies with men/current strength, losses, morale and ammunition.
- AI ON is green and AI OFF red. DEF/BAL/OFF use red for inactive and green for the active doctrine.
- Battalion order buttons are compact and show the pending point-order in green: ANGRIB HER, FORSVAR HER, RYK FREM, TILBAGETRÆK, SAML and STOP/HOLD.
- Company HUD is divided into ENHEDSINFO, AI/DOKTRIN, SKYDNING, ORDRER/BEVÆGELSE and FORMATION.
- Company fire controls are grouped as HOLD/CLOSE/MED/LONG with red inactive / green active state.
- Fighting withdrawal controls are grouped as -> MED, -> LONG and -> UD, with the active common withdrawal mode shown green when all selected companies share it.
- TVANG, CHARGE and STOP are in the company movement/order group. Forced march and active charge use green state feedback.
- STOP is a hard direct company command: AI is turned OFF, the manual route is cleared, fighting withdrawal is cancelled, an active infantry charge is ended, forced march is disabled and the unit receives OrderHold().
- Formation controls are LINJE, KOLONNE and SQUARE. SQUARE now has a visible company-HUD button and uses the existing F29 square implementation rather than a duplicate formation model.
- Formation buttons are direct company authority just like the existing F/C/Q hotkeys; selecting a formation switches local company AI OFF so the higher mission cannot immediately overwrite the player's formation choice.
- Danish company display naming is normalized to 1. KOMPAGNI through 8. KOMPAGNI. Legacy internal prototype identifiers such as 1. Regiment / 5. Regiment / 2. Regiment / 3. Regiment are retained internally for compatibility, while Unity GameObject/display names are corrected at runtime.
- The unified HUD consumes pointer events in the existing 90 px bottom command area after processing its buttons, preventing hidden legacy IMGUI buttons underneath from double-firing.
- Backup before this revision: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f29b-pre-09f29c-hud.

Retained from v00.00.09f29b - Battlefield scale + attack AI hardening
- Tactical battlefield is doubled in both linear dimensions from 2880 x 1920 m to 5760 x 3840 m. This is 2x width, 2x depth and 4x area versus f29a.
- The road and river are generated across the complete enlarged battlefield instead of ending at the previous map bounds.
- Vegetation coverage expands to the larger battlefield while retaining the same central farm/bridge QA terrain.
- Terrain mesh segment count remains 576 x 384 to avoid a 4x triangle increase; the larger map therefore uses coarser terrain cells away from the detailed central test area.
- The tactical scenario now retains two Prussian company-scale formations: 8th Regiment and 18th Regiment. Both are normalized to the 190-man company QA scale.
- 18th Regiment no longer executes the old one-off flank waypoint from the pre-regimental test scenario; it enters a normal hostile attack mission.
- Attack frontage planning no longer writes OrderMove/OrderHold every 0.25 seconds. Physical movement is left to the current authority owner.
- The frontage layer now ignores a company while its OfficerAIController component is deliberately disabled by a Major/higher command layer.
- The frontage layer also leaves units under the F26 under-fire reaction untouched.
- Explicit AttackTarget missions are authoritative; the frontage layer no longer replaces them with whichever enemy happens to be nearest that frame.
- AttackNearest becomes a sticky explicit target after entering the engagement envelope, preventing formations from pivoting repeatedly between nearby enemies.
- Hard-coded old attack-slot clamps of approximately +/-170 x +/-110 m are removed; advisory slot calculations use the real battlefield bounds.
- Approach formation policy now respects the same authority locks, so it cannot change a Major-owned company from Line to Column while the Major is placing it.
- Regimental AttackHere role assignment is corrected: when doctrine calls for one battalion forward plus reserve, the battalion nearest the objective remains the front/assault battalion and the farther battalion becomes reserve. Total-travel permutation can no longer make the nearest battalion retreat while the distant battalion passes it.
- Offensive front+flank assignment uses the same nearest-battalion-as-front rule.
- Retains f29a terrain-following command hierarchy lines, company -> Major visibility, Major -> Oberstløjtnant visibility, officer target-area cursor and full company-footprint river endpoint protection.

v00.00.09f29 - Infantry square / cavalry-threat foundation
- Adds first runtime KARRÉ/SQUARE state for Danish infantry companies without replacing the stable Line/Column movement kernel.
- Q toggles selected Danish companies into manual KARRÉ; F reforms to Line and C leaves square for Column through the existing command system.
- Manual KARRÉ is a direct player order: selected company AI is switched OFF so current Major mission is released under the existing authority rules.
- Square formation is physically visualised by redistributing the existing 1:1 infantry render positions around a two-rank perimeter rather than teleporting a separate placeholder icon.
- Square formation time depends on current cohesion. First QA window is roughly 5.2-8.4 seconds.
- A square cannot fire while formation is still forming.
- When ready, square shows all-direction CLOSE/MEDIUM/LONG range rings instead of the normal forward-only fire fan.
- First square firing model represents one active face at a time and reduces effective fire output with a 0.42 square-fire factor so KARRÉ does not become a 360-degree full-company volley.
- Square expands the company collision footprint to approximately 20.5 x 20.5 m while active.
- F29 exposes a mounted-threat API for F30: ReportMountedThreat(company, source, distance, closingSpeed, relativeStrength, charging).
- AI ON infantry may automatically FORM SQUARE when mounted threat score exceeds the first QA threshold. Distance, closing speed, relative strength and explicit charge state contribute to the score.
- Automatic square suspends physical movement while preserving the parent Major/Regimental mission. When the mounted threat clears, square reforms and the stored parent mission may resume.
- AI OFF does not auto-square from mounted threat; direct player authority remains literal.
- Shift+Q is a temporary QA hook that reports a synthetic cavalry charge against selected AI ON infantry so auto-square can be tested before F30 creates the first real cavalry unit.
- Future mounted melee/charge code can query IsInSquare(), IsSquareReady() and GetMountedChargeDefenseMultiplier(). A ready square currently exposes x0.34 mounted charge effectiveness; forming square x0.72. These are QA values, not final historical tuning.
- Console telemetry prefixes: SQUARE-09F29 and SQUARE-THREAT-09F29.
