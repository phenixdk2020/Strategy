v00.00.09f30k - Auto March Column + RMB Facing + Split Dragon Selection + Higher HQ AI HUD
- Backup before F30K: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f30j-pre-09f30k-auto-march-column at F30J HEAD 6fe686f6650a9be10de4bd568868fe60602f8e17.
- FIX: manual cavalry movement again has automatic march formation policy even when Officer AI is OFF.
- Long mounted MOVE enters 4-abreast Column at >=140m remaining when no valid enemy is inside Long range.
- Automatic march deploys back to 4-rank Line at <=90m remaining or immediately when a valid enemy comes inside current 100m Long range.
- 140/90m hysteresis prevents Line/Column oscillation around one threshold.
- Bridge routing remains higher priority and still forces 2-abreast bridge/defile column.
- Manual formation buttons now create an explicit formation override; automatic march policy does not silently replace a formation the player deliberately selected.
- HOLD clears the manual formation override; enabling Officer AI also returns formation policy to delegated control.
- RMB input now matches infantry semantics: right mouse down sets destination, hold+drag sets explicit final facing, release commits MOVE + facing.
- Cavalry turns to the explicit final facing at the destination before completing the move order.
- Destination ghost uses the explicit dragged facing, not only travel direction.
- A dismounted Dragon no longer uses one giant selection rectangle covering the empty space between horses and combat line.
- Dismounted selection is split into combat-line box + smaller horse-holder box + thin connecting line.
- SID AF remains ~2.25s; STIG OP/remount is doubled to ~4.5s.
- Brigade and Division HUD now expose AI ON/OFF plus DEF/BAL/OFF doctrine controls in the same command language as Regiment/Battalion.
- Brigade/Division AI defaults OFF. The toggle is a real command-authority state: attached cavalry Officer AI waits when its current parent HQ AI is OFF.
- Cavalry attached to Regiment likewise requires Regimental AI ON; cavalry attached to Major A/B continues to obey the corresponding Battalion AI.
- Higher-HQ orders pass the selected Brigade/Division doctrine into the Regimental execution pipeline and mark delegation autonomous when higher AI is ON.
- OOB AI column now shows ON/OFF for Brigade and Division.
