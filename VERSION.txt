v00.00.09f26 - Under-fire reaction / mission suspension
- AI ON company moving under a parent/local AI mission now reacts to a fresh hostile-fire event when a valid enemy is inside its own Long/MaximumRange.
- Reaction is SUSPEND, not DELETE: the company stops, deploys Line, faces the local threat and returns fire when its existing fire policy permits.
- HOLD FIRE remains authoritative and is never silently broken by the reaction layer.
- A shorter CLOSE/MEDIUM fire policy may be temporarily widened to LONG solely for the local return-fire reaction; the previous fire policy is restored afterward.
- Parent Major mission remains stored. After a quiet period the reaction layer releases control and the normal parent/local Officer AI may resume the original mission.
- AI OFF remains literal player authority: B-282 does not auto-cancel a direct player movement order.
- Active bridge passage is a critical exception: reaction is deferred until the bridge route has cleared, avoiding formations stopping in the bridge throat.
- Console telemetry prefix: UNDER-FIRE-09F26.

v00.00.09f26 - Roadmap gate after Regiment control
