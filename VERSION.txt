v00.00.09f25 - Local Captain authority after manual AI ON
- Fixes the regression where a company detached by a manual player order could resurrect its OLD Major assignment when AI was switched ON again.
- AI ON after a manual order now means local Captain AI, not automatic return to the previous Major destination.
- The stale lastAssignments entry is invalidated before the Major controller can reapply it.
- If a PlayerCommander manual movement route is still active, AIEnabled remains ON but OfficerAI Update is temporarily paused until that route completes.
- Only a NEW Major order may reclaim the company into battalion command.
- Console telemetry: AI-AUTHORITY-09F25.

v00.00.09f25 - Infantry CHARGE + melee approach
- Selected Danish companies receive an explicit CHARGE [V] command and enemy target-pick mode.
- CHARGE is not normal OrderAttack: it ignores musket stop range and closes toward physical contact.
- Direct player charge releases Major authority and sets company AI OFF.
- Charging infantry uses HOLD FIRE, Line in open approach and temporary Column where the existing bridge router requires it.
- Prototype charge speed: Denmark 4.80 m/s, Prussia 5.00 m/s; charge adds extra cohesion cost while closing.
- At approximately 7.2 m contact, movement stops and the existing PrototypeMeleeCombatManager owns melee contact/casualty resolution.
- Previous fire policy is restored when the charge ends because the target breaks/routes or the attacker routes.
- Existing terrain/bridge routing remains authoritative during the approach.
- Console telemetry: CHARGE-09F25.

v00.00.09f25 - Dynamic Major HQ advance / command zone MVP
- Major HQ no longer has to remain indefinitely at its original position while an AI-controlled battalion advances.
- With Major AI ON and active ATTACK/ADVANCE/DEFEND mission, HQ reviews its physical position every 2.5 seconds.
- When subordinate companies have moved far enough forward, HQ attempts a safe relocation in bounds, normally roughly 145-165 m behind battalion centre/front direction.
- HQ relocation preserves the Major mission; it changes HQ position only.
- The first HQ movement pass only accepts a clear straight route and refuses relocation through known blockers/open river. HQ-specific bridge/path planning remains later work.
- Prototype command bands: IN COMMAND <= 320 m, EXTENDED <= 450 m, OUT OF COMMAND > 450 m.
- EXTENDED/OUT OF COMMAND add reaction/AI decision delay only. They do NOT reduce musket accuracy or weapon range.
- Selecting the Major shows 320/450 m command-zone rings; selecting a subordinate company shows its current command band and distance to HQ in the bottom HUD.
- Console telemetry: HQ-ZONE-09F25.
