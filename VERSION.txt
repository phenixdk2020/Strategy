v00.00.09l4 - Company screen-space selection
- 1:1 ordinary soldiers remain GPU-instanced and have no individual colliders.
- A new screen-space company hit test projects each company tactical footprint into the camera view.
- A short LMB click anywhere inside the visible company footprint selects that company even if the ray never hits the lightweight company collider.
- Shift and Ctrl semantics remain add/toggle.
- 09l2 remains owner of LMB drag/box selection; 09l4 only corrects short-click selection after the ordinary 09l2 click path.
- Whole-regiment PlayerCommander selection remains available through the existing regiment/HQ path.
- Console marker: SELECT-09L4|Installed=True|Mode=ScreenSpaceCompanyFootprint.

v00.00.09l4 - Company combat authority
- The old Regiment object is no longer allowed to fire a regiment-wide volley from the mounted HQ/pivot while companies are the visible tactical formations.
- Active company-parent regiments are forced to HoldFire in this QA gate.
- Parent regiment range cones are hidden in company-control mode.
- This is intentionally a safe interim state: company-level fire/LOS/ammo/casualty resolution is not faked before it is implemented properly.
- Existing combat code is not deleted or rewritten; its previous fire policy is retained for restoration when the authority layer is removed.
- Console marker: COMBAT-AUTH-09L4|Installed=True|ParentRegimentFire=False.

v00.00.09l4 - Company guidons / identity markers
- The full historical national/regimental standards remain at Regimental HQ.
- Every active company receives two smaller tactical guidons attached to its company tactical centre:
  1. national identity marker,
  2. regiment/company identity marker.
- Danish company national marker uses Dannebrog colours; the company marker carries regiment Roman identity plus company number.
- Prussian QA companies receive equivalent national/regiment-company markers.
- These are tactical readability markers, not a claim that every historical company carried a full regimental colour.
- Guidons move and rotate with the company tactical centre.
- Console marker: GUIDON-09L4|Installed=True|PerCompany=2.

Retained v00.00.09l3 - Reduced QA battle
- Temporary scale-down while company selection, movement and formation geometry are stabilized.
- Active infantry:
  * Denmark: 1. Infanteri-Regiment, approximately 1,540 men, 8 companies.
  * Prussia: 8th Regiment (QA), approximately 2,460 men, 12 companies.
- Active infantry total: approximately 4,000 men.
- Active company tactical centres: 20 total.
- 11. Infanteri-Regiment and 18th Regiment remain defined in the OOB model but hidden from the active QA battle.
- Brigade reserve/commit AI remains paused in this two-regiment QA gate.

Retained v00.00.09l2 - Company tactical control
- Every company has a lightweight tactical centre; ordinary soldiers remain GPU-instanced.
- LMB click/Shift/Ctrl and LMB box-select support Danish company selection.
- RMB moves selected companies; Alt+RMB appends a waypoint; RMB drag sets final facing.
- F = Line, C = Column, H = Hold, Z/X rotate company facing.
- Double-clicking a company returns to whole-regiment selection.
- Manual company orders clear the parent regiment route, switch parent Officer AI OFF and set the parent regiment Hold.

Retained formation geometry
- 1:1 infantry is anchored to individual company tactical centres rather than one regiment-wide pivot.
- Company Line = 3 ranks, approximately 0.52 m file spacing / 0.76 m rank spacing.
- Company Column = 8 files across.
- Four companies form a battalion line; battalions occupy separate template rows.
- Company casualty slots remain deterministically distributed to prevent artificial tail/L-shaped losses.

Retained OOB / HQ / standards / arms baseline
- Historical Danish working OOB remains 7. Brigade: 1. + 11. Infanteri-Regiment in the data model.
- Prussian 8th + 18th pair remains explicitly QA pending source lock.
- Regimental HQ = 3 mounted staff figures; Brigade HQ = 6 mounted staff figures.
- Full dual standards remain national/army flag + regimental identity flag at Regimental HQ.
- Danish battery baseline = 8 guns / approximately 190 personnel.
- Cavalry manpower, serviceable horses and mounted effective remain separate values.

Retained movement baseline
- Navigation V3 remains whole-regiment steering authority.
- 09i1 Farmhouse/Barn detour continuation remains active.
- 09h4 manual AI-OFF regiment route recovery remains active.
- Trees/fence posts remain soft/pass-through; Farmhouse/Barn hard; river bridge-only.
- Company movement remains lightweight direct movement without independent V3 obstacle routing in this QA gate.

Known transition constraints
- Parent regiment fire is intentionally suppressed in 09l4 until proper company-level fire resolution is implemented.
- Company-level LOS, fire eligibility, ammo and casualty targeting remain the next combat architecture gate.
- Unity Input Manager deprecation warning remains non-blocking; Input System migration remains separate.
