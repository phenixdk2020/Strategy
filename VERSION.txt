v00.00.09f29x - Square fire + OOB selection hardening
- Backup created before changes: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f29w-pre-09f29x-square-oob-fix.
- Adds Assets/Scripts/PrototypeSquareOobHardening09F29X.cs.
- Fixes the wrong yellow Line/Column selection footprint remaining visible while a company is in Square.
- Keeps the actual SquareOutline and four Square fire sectors as the Square visual authority.
- Fixes false smoke / false volley feedback caused by F29V parking Regiment.nextFireTime roughly 3600 seconds into the future.
- Legacy Regiment auto-fire is gated with temporary HoldFire only during Regiment.Update; the player's actual fire policy is restored before F29V sector fire runs.
- F29V parking is removed again in the same frame before legacy combat telemetry / F7 smoke detection can interpret it as a shot.

- If a real Square sector volley fired, a normal reload timer is preserved so existing smoke / hit / ammunition feedback can still detect the real shot.
- OOB-derived Company/Major/Regimental selection is preserved through bottom-HUD clicks and officer-order placement/commit.
- Selection persistence does not move the camera and does not change OOB single-click/double-click semantics.
- No HQ depth, minimap, NATO semantic zoom, river safety, charge or movement-owner rules are changed.

v00.00.09f29w - HQ depth / rear-echelon guard
- Adds PrototypeHqDepthGuard09F29W.
- Fixes AI-controlled Major and Regimental HQs being allowed to drift too close to or ahead of their subordinate front.
- Actual nearest active enemy direction is used as the tactical forward axis instead of relying only on the last order point.
- Major HQ minimum rear depth: 120 m; preferred correction depth: 175 m.
- Regimental HQ minimum rear depth: 220 m behind Major midpoint; preferred correction depth: 285 m.
- Existing river/formation-endpoint safety remains authoritative.
- Manual HQ authority is preserved when AI is OFF.

v00.00.09f29v - Tactical map, semantic zoom, OOB status and Square corrections
- Fixed whole-battlefield tactical map with moving camera viewport.
- Explicit II/III HQ markers on tactical map.
- Company NATO counters continuous from 175 m; 3D + NATO from 175-314 m; NATO-only from 315 m upward.
- Tactical OOB statuses include KLAR, RYKKER, FORSVAR, RESERVE, FLANKE, SQUARE, KAMP, UNDER ILD, CHARGE, MELEE and ROUT.
- Four 90-degree Square fire sectors require a real valid enemy target before volley.
