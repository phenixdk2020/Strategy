v00.00.09f29g - Contact combat + facing orders + unified HUD + river continuity
- Adds full selection command-chain visibility: selected company -> owning Major -> Oberstløjtnant; selected Major -> own companies + Oberstløjtnant; selected Oberstløjtnant -> both Majors -> all companies.
- Adds company contact override during parent AttackHere missions: a company drops the march leg and engages when an enemy enters its currently selected fire-policy range.
- CLOSE/MED/LONG therefore become the actual contact trigger. HOLD does not auto-open fire.
- Preserves the company fire-policy preference across higher AttackHere orders instead of silently forcing LONG.
- Disables the F29E coordinated attack-slot writer so it cannot compete with F27 parent movement and local Captain contact authority.
- Adds unified click/drag officer point orders: click sets objective; drag from the objective sets explicit facing; click without drag keeps automatic facing.
- A new higher officer order cancels any active company-level fighting withdrawal under the affected battalion before applying the new order.
- Adds one authoritative F29G HUD for Regiment, Battalion/Major and Company/Kaptajn using the approved F29F visual language.
- F29C and F29F overlay components are disabled at runtime by F29G; old F27/F28 command components remain state engines behind the opaque input-consuming panel.
- Company HUD retains fire policy, withdrawal bands, forced march, charge, stop and Line/Column/Square controls.
- Adds a continuous river-water ribbon from map edge to map edge, including underneath the bridge; navigation rules are unchanged.
- Backup: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f29f-pre-09f29g-contact-ai-hud-chain.
