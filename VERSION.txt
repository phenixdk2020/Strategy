v00.00.09i1 - V3 hard-obstacle detour continuation fix
- QA log from a direct player-controlled AI-OFF group move confirmed 1. Regiment can enter a repeat loop around a Farmhouse.
- The captured log contains thousands of repeated NAV-V3 Detour=True entries for the same Farmhouse centre and the same detour point.
- Root cause: after reaching the first detour point, the same hard obstacle can still block the final route. Base V3 finishes the reached detour leg and can immediately select the same/near-identical point again instead of continuing around the obstacle.
- 09h4 manual route recovery correctly detected no progress and inserted a bypass, but V3 route sanitisation/goal adjustment could move that bypass and the base detour loop could resume.
- New PrototypeNavigationV3DetourContinuation09I1 runs immediately after base V3 and only acts when V3 is already in a Farmhouse/Barn detour and the current detour point has effectively been reached while the real goal is still distant.
- The layer chooses a new safe continuation point around the SAME active hard obstacle, validates the local leg with V3 Column navigation/path penalties, rejects local river-crossing candidates, and writes the continuation back into V3's existing NavigationState.
- Navigation V3 remains the steering owner. 09i1 does not move/teleport the regiment and does not create a second pathfinding authority.
- Continuation legs are bounded to 10 per active obstacle and emit explicit NAV-V3-09I1 telemetry.
- Expected success telemetry: NAV-V3-09I1|Unit=...|Continuation=True|Obstacle=Farmhouse|FromVia=...|NextVia=...
- This fix applies to Farmhouse and Barn hard obstacles; decorative trees and individual FencePosts remain soft/pass-through from 09h4.

v00.00.09i - Soldier Visual Pass 2 / Big Graphics Buff
- New PrototypeSoldierVisualPass09I is presentation-only and overlays the retained 09h uniform-data system.
- Legacy primitive soldier renderers are hidden at runtime; Regiment simulation objects, formation slots and colliders remain authoritative and unchanged.
- Representative infantry use lightweight articulated procedural rigs with improved human proportions: tapered torso, coat skirts, separate legs, boots, arms, hands, neck/head and faction-specific headgear.
- Danish infantry receive a distinct cap/visor/band silhouette; Prussian infantry receive a stronger Pickelhaube dome/band/spike/visor silhouette.
- Equipment pass adds backpack, crossed straps, cartridge box, optional canteen variants, improved rifle stock/barrel/bayonet and metal/wood material separation.
- Officer representative receives sash, shoulder boards and sword.
- Standard-bearer representative receives sash/pole grip and carries no rifle.
- Six deterministic visual variants subtly alter scale/equipment so the formation does not read as exact cloned figures.
- Simple visual posing is added without animation-controller dependencies: march arm/leg swing, stationary ready pose and brief firing pose driven only from already-existing visual/combat state.
- Close-detail LOD hides small equipment details beyond approximately 135 m camera distance while retaining readable body/head/rifle silhouettes.
- Uniform Designer remains authoritative for coat, trousers, headgear, trim, straps, equipment and skin colours; 09i materials re-sync live to the existing per-regiment profile.
- No movement, destination, combat, morale, cohesion, selection or Officer AI state is written by the 09i graphics layer.

v00.00.09i - Regimental Standard Visual Pass 2
- PrototypeRegimentalStandards09I hides the old 09g QA standard renderers while retaining the underlying system as rollback/reference code.
- Each regiment receives a larger procedural two-sided cloth mesh with animated wind deformation.
- Standard includes improved wooden pole, ferrule, spear-style finial, cords, two animated ribbons and fringe detail.
- Danish and Prussian standards retain distinct national prototype devices and synchronize flag primary/secondary plus ribbon colours with the Uniform Designer profile.
- Flag animation is presentation-only and has no morale/combat effect.
- Historical regimental flag art remains a later source-validation task; 09i is a visual-quality prototype, not final historical artwork.

Known warnings / non-blocking migration items
- Existing yellow CS0618 FindObjectsByType/FindObjectsSortMode warnings are Unity 6.6 API deprecation warnings. They do not cause the movement stall and remain scheduled for a separate compatibility cleanup.
