v00.00.09f24 - Selected company can inspect its Major destination
- Major mission path/footprint visuals are no longer visible only while the Major itself is selected.
- If one or more subordinate companies are selected and they still belong to the active Major mission, each selected company shows its own current path and destination footprint.
- Selecting a company does not detach it; only a direct manual order does that.
- Manual AI-OFF companies still lose the Major mission visual as intended.

v00.00.09f24 - New Major order is a hard replacement
- Every newly created Major mission set receives a new execution identity, even if the player repeats the same order at the same map point.
- On a new Major order, stale direct-player routes/ghosts are cleared, old direct attack targets are removed and the old local Officer-AI mission is flushed before the new destination is written.
- The new order therefore replaces the previous live execution instead of coexisting with it.
- Console telemetry: HQ-COMMIT-09F24.

v00.00.09f24 - Destination box is authoritative
- A Major-owned company may not mark itself arrived merely because an enemy enters fire range while the displayed destination box is still several metres away.
- Exact arrival tolerance is approximately 4.5 m from the assigned company slot.
- While travelling, local Officer-AI Update is temporarily paused but AIEnabled remains ON.
- The strategic goal is restored only when no active movement destination exists, so bridge/building steering waypoints are not overwritten.
- When the exact slot is reached, local Officer AI is released again.
- This fixes the observed case where companies stopped short of the yellow/blue destination boxes.
