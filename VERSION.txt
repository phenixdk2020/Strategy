v00.00.09f17 - Manual multi-company Line order fix
- Reproduced QA issue: two selected ~48 m companies were previously placed only 22 m center-to-center and therefore overlapped.
- Added PrototypeGroupLineSpacing09F17.
- Normal group Line placement now uses 60 m center spacing: ~48 m company frontage plus ~12 m interval.
- The existing PlayerCommander route objects are corrected after the order is created, preserving route/pathfinding/ghost behavior instead of replacing the movement system.
- Group destination preview is also expanded to the true multi-company frontage.
- Expected result: 2, 3 or 4 selected companies line up SIDE BY SIDE, not on top of each other.

v00.00.09f17 - Formation-level attack lanes
- Reproduced second QA issue: multiple companies ordered to ATTACK the same enemy all chased the same target transform and collapsed on top of each other.
- Added PrototypeFormationAttackLanes09F17.
- When 2+ Danish companies share the same attack target, the order is converted to one common firing line with separate company lanes.
- Lane spacing is 60 m, matching the side-by-side group Line rule.
- The attack line stops at the shortest active fire-policy range among the attacking companies (bounded to 28-88 m), so all companies can engage without converging on the enemy center.
- Each company moves to its own lane in Line formation; Regiment.OrderMove clears the shared forced target that caused the overlap.
- This applies to both manual multi-selection ATTACK and Major-issued attacks once multiple companies share the same target.

v00.00.09f17 - Four-company Danish battalion
- Scenario now creates/keeps four Danish company-scale maneuver units under the Major.
- UI names are 1. Kompagni, 2. Kompagni, 3. Kompagni and 4. Kompagni; internal legacy IDs remain 1.Regiment, 5.Regiment, 2.Regiment and 3.Regiment.
- All four Danish companies plus the single Prussian test company are normalized to 190 men by the company-scale layer.
- Initial Danish company positions are spaced side by side at z=-90,-30,+30,+90.

v00.00.09f17 - Major reserve and flanking MVP
- Added PrototypeMajorBattalion09F17 as the authoritative four-company HQ behavior/UI layer after the physical 09f15 Major + three horses has spawned.
- Major still starts HQ AI OFF.
- With HQ AI OFF, player Major orders use the four companies directly.
- With HQ AI ON, attack/defend decisions can hold one fresh company as reserve while three companies form the primary front.
- Reserve selection favors the freshest/most cohesive available company.
- If battalion strength/cohesion are good and the enemy is close enough, the Major may stage the reserve on a flank.
- On ATTACK, a flank reserve can commit against a nearby enemy after reaching a useful flank position.
- On DEFEND, a flank reserve can perform a local counterattack if the enemy closes sufficiently and reserve strength/cohesion remain adequate.
- This is a first tactical decision MVP, not the final historical officer-stat/personality/order-delay model.
