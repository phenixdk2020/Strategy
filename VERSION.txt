Built-in modules enabled: IMGUI, Particle System, Physics, Audio
Previous gate: v00.00.08 reload/experience, 0-hit volley, 'Ramte N' and simple casualty visual retained
Scenario: Danish test regiments are defenders; Prussian test regiments are attackers
Battlefield: doubled from 180x120 to 360x240 with expanded camera bounds and greater starting separation
Fire geometry: directional 120-degree forward fire fan follows regiment facing; no 360-degree firing ring
Range bands: Close <= 50% EffectiveRange; Medium <= EffectiveRange; Long <= MaximumRange
Range fan visibility: Close/Medium arcs and outer Long fan are terrain-following, unlit, thicker and higher-contrast so no side disappears into uneven ground
Fire policy: HOLD / CLOSE / MEDIUM / LONG determines when a regiment may open fire
Accuracy: TEST tuning now compensates the legacy kernel so Close/Medium/Long multipliers are applied exactly once instead of stacking on top of the old distance curve
Combat tuning menu: F9 or KAMP F9 opens runtime sliders for base hit chance per firing man, Close/Medium/Long factors, firing fraction, morale/cohesion accuracy floors, starting ammunition rounds per man and losses per visible casualty body
Combat tuning defaults: base hit chance 1.40%, Close x1.75, Medium x1.00, Long x0.30, 58% firing fraction, morale floor 72%, cohesion floor 68%, 60 starting rounds per man and 8 losses per visible casualty body
Morale/cohesion accuracy revision: morale and cohesion still reduce hit probability, but accuracy no longer collapses toward zero merely because prolonged close-range fire has reduced both values while hundreds of men remain formed
Volley diagnostics: every detected volley writes VOLLEY-LOG to Unity Console with battle time, shooter, target, distance, range band, fire policy, strength, firing men, morale, cohesion, quality multiplier, base hit chance, range multiplier, expected hits, resolved hits, target strength and ammunition before/after
Fire eligibility MVP: target must be inside selected fire-policy range and inside the forward fire arc
Reload: weapon profile base reload + bounded regiment Experience modifier retained from v00.00.08
Volley feedback revision: TEST layer detects every resolved volley and shows temporary Ramte N including Ramte 0; feedback lifetime increased for readability
Ammunition TEST model: starting rounds per man are configurable; one volley consumes one round per man; display is rounds per man, not total cartridges; no automatic regeneration
Officer assignment: every prototype regiment receives an OfficerProfile + OfficerAIController at runtime
Officer core stats: Leadership, Inspiration, Tactical Skill, Initiative, Staff/Command Skill, Discipline/Obedience, Aggressiveness/Caution, Composure/Nerve
Officer background: Experience is separate and primarily reduces decision noise
Player delegation: I toggles AI UNIT ON/OFF for selected Danish regiment(s); A remains camera-left in WASD
Officer AI doctrine menu: DEFENSIVE / BALANCED / OFFENSIVE applies to selected Danish regiment(s)
Commander aggression intent: 0-100 slider biases cautious/aggressive execution, while officer Aggressiveness remains dominant (70% officer / 30% order before doctrine bias)
Fire discipline menu: HOLD / CLOSE / MEDIUM / LONG applies to selected Danish regiment(s)
Battle HUD revision: selected/mouse-over info is compact and includes current/initial strength, Tab/losses, Ammo rounds per man, weapon/reload/formation, fire ranges, morale/cohesion and compact Officer AI state
Command UI safe-area revision: bottom command bar is two rows, 58 px high, inset 50 px horizontally and 42 px from the lower edge so it remains visible in slightly zoomed Unity Game view
Time HUD safe-area revision: date/time plus PAUSE/x0.5/x1/x2/x5/x20 are centred 20 px below the upper edge; Space and 0/1/2/3/4 remain shortcuts
UI input isolation: clicks inside time controls, combat tuning panel or bottom command bar do not issue battlefield selection/move/attack orders
Multi-selection: Ctrl+left-click or Shift+left-click adds/removes Danish regiments from the current selection
Formation control: manual F/C selects line/column; Officer AI may use column for longer approach marches and returns to line near engagement based on Tactical Skill and distance
Single-unit move/facing: right-click location is the destination; while RMB is held the yellow destination footprint itself rotates live with the mouse drag, with a short facing guide showing the final direction before release
Destination formation rule: movement may be executed in column, but the destination ghost footprint and final deployed state are LINE by default
Multi-unit formation-line order: with several units selected, hold right mouse and drag a line; selected regiments are assigned evenly along the line and drag direction determines facing
Waypoint routes: Alt+right-click appends a waypoint; repeat for multiple waypoints; Alt+right-drag on a waypoint sets an explicit final facing for the route
Waypoint obedience revision: PlayerCommander executes after Officer AI and reasserts the active player route every frame; route arrival tolerance is synchronized so Officer AI can no longer stop just outside a waypoint and strand the route
Order ghosts: route ghost follows all remaining waypoints and the final destination footprint shows final LINE formation and facing; Z/X can rotate final ghost facing without cancelling movement
Facing order: Z rotates selected regiment(s) 15 degrees left; X rotates 15 degrees right; matching compact buttons remain in the bottom command bar
Casualty visuals: bodies accumulate at the actual formation position as losses occur even while units remain stationary and exchange fire; density is runtime-configurable, default one visible body per eight losses
Enemy AI: Prussian officers start Offensive with order aggression 65 and Medium fire policy; 18th uses a flank/approach waypoint before reassessing attack
Enemy behaviour rule: attackers close to officer/order preferred range, deploy to line near engagement, may stabilise under morale pressure, and do not receive combat-stat cheats
Difficulty: F6 Easy, F7 Normal, F8 Hard; affects enemy AI reaction/decision noise only, not combat stats or hidden knowledge
AI telemetry: AI-DIAG exposes unit, officer, doctrine, order aggression, effective aggression, fire policy, mission, task and reason
Pause semantics: AI, movement, waypoint movement, reload, combat and battle clock stop together; camera/UI remain usable
Slow tactical mode: x0.5 gives twice the real-world reaction time per unit of simulation time without changing combat stats
