v00.00.09f10 - Enemy inside Long => stay in Line
- Automatic march-column policy now uses each unit's actual MaximumRange as the combat deployment boundary.
- If the nearest valid enemy is within Long/MaximumRange, the unit must remain in or deploy to Line.
- Changing fire policy LONG -> MEDIUM/CLOSE does not by itself allow Column when the enemy is already inside Long.
- An explicit ANGREB order inside Long therefore manoeuvres in Line rather than reforming into Column.
- Active bridge routing is the current explicit exception: a legal river crossing may force Column until the far staging point has been cleared.

v00.00.09f10 - Controlled fighting withdrawal
- Added three bottom-bar withdrawal controls: TRÆK -> MED, TRÆK -> LONG and TRÆK -> UD.
- MED target is current EffectiveRange (currently 70 m in QA).
- LONG target is approximately two thirds into the Medium->Maximum interval (currently about 90 m).
- UD target is MaximumRange + 15 m (currently about 115 m).
- Fighting withdrawal is controlled combat movement, not rout.
- Unit remains in Line and faces the nearest valid threat.
- Sequence alternates stationary covering-fire pauses with approximately 10 m backward steps.
- Backward speed is approximately 1.70 m/s.
- During the actual backstep fire policy is temporarily HOLD; during stationary covering-fire phases it temporarily uses LONG permission.
- The previous fire policy is restored when the order completes.
- Explicit withdrawal takes priority over Officer AI. Officer AI is left OFF when the order completes so an Offensive officer cannot immediately undo the retreat; player may re-enable AI deliberately.
- Fighting withdrawal does not automatically reverse-march through the bridge. If the next local backstep would enter open water, the withdrawal halts instead.
