v00.00.09f29z - Square face fire + directional smoke
- Backup created before changes: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f29y-pre-09f29z-square-fire-smoke.
- Adds Assets/Scripts/PrototypeSquareFireSmoke09F29Z.cs.
- F29V remains visual authority for Square outline and four range sectors, but its reflected full-company FireVolley path is suppressed.
- Each Square face (FRONT/RIGHT/REAR/LEFT) owns a separate reload clock.
- Each face resolves approximately 25% of normal company volley firepower and only fires at a valid enemy inside that face's 90-degree sector and current fire-policy range.
- Crop-field target visibility/accuracy modifiers still apply to Square fire.
- Only the face that actually fires emits black-powder smoke; smoke is positioned and oriented along that physical side of the Square.
- Two or more faces may fire independently when valid enemies exist in different sectors.
- No valid target means no volley, no smoke and no RAMMER 0 feedback.
- PrototypeCombatStatusManager gains RegisterExternalVolley for real non-Regiment-timer volleys.
- Square face ammunition is accumulated fractionally: four 25% face volleys consume one average round/man, instead of charging one full round/man for every face.
- Existing F29X legacy-auto-fire gate remains active; no second physical movement or fire owner is introduced.
