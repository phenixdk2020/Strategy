v00.01.00c - Gate C: Company-driven 1:1 GPU renderer
- Builds directly on accepted 00.01.00b Gate A+B architecture.
- Stable UnitID/OOB and eight independent TacticalCompanyEntity00B objects are unchanged.
- Active QA force remains one Danish battalion and one Prussian battalion: four Companies per side.
- Active manpower remains Denmark 759 + Prussia 812 = 1571 men.
- The solid Gate B QA footprint renderer is hidden; its collider and selection outline remain as the Company selection footprint.
- One visible soldier is rendered for every active Company manpower record: 1:1 visual ratio.
- Ordinary soldiers are rendered with Graphics.DrawMeshInstanced; no GameObject/MonoBehaviour/Collider/NavMeshAgent/Animator per soldier.
- Company remains the sole source of world pose for its rendered soldiers.
- Renderer is presentation-only and writes no movement, combat, selection or OOB state.
- Gate C QA soldier is built from shared instanced meshes: legs, torso, head, headgear, pack and rifle.
- Danish and Prussian QA uniform palettes are visually distinct; palette is not yet the final historical uniform-art gate.
- Line layout remains three ranks with 0.52 m file spacing and 0.76 m rank spacing.
- Tiny deterministic positional variance prevents a perfectly synthetic visual grid without changing tactical frontage.
- Company-level distance LOD is active from the start: Full <=260 m, Medium <=520 m, Far <=850 m.
- Full detail renders legs, torso, head, headgear, pack and rifle.
- Medium detail renders torso, head, headgear and rifle.
- Far detail renders torso and headgear.
- Company instance arrays are persistent; no per-frame batch-array allocation is used.
- Gate C performance overlay shows visible soldier count, smoothed FPS/ms, draw-call count and Company LOD distribution.
- Existing Gate B selection owner is intentionally unchanged; Gate C only covers its old status panel with current Gate C text.
- RMB still does not move units. Movement/navigation remains Gate D.
- Company combat remains absent. Fire/reload/ammo/casualties remain Gate E.
- Officer AI, Brigade AI, artillery and cavalry remain inactive.
