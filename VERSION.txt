v00.00.09f27 - Two battalions / two Majors
- Adds a second Danish battalion with four additional company-scale infantry units.
- Danish hierarchy now contains 8 companies split 4 + 4 under Major A and Major B.
- Both Majors are physical selectable HQs with 3 horses each.
- Both Majors use the SAME PrototypeRegimentHierarchy09F27 controller, UI, mission planner, reserve/flank logic and authority rules.
- The legacy single-Major control stack is disabled at runtime after F27 installs to avoid two competing authority systems.
- Both Major HUDs use the same 90 px full-width bottom HUD and six commands: ANGRIB HER / FORSVAR HER / RYK FREM / TILBAGETRÆK / SAML / HOLD.
- Each Major has AI ON/OFF plus DEF/BAL/OFF doctrine.
- New Major orders reclaim subordinate companies and set company AI ON; direct company orders may still detach companies through the established AI OFF/manual authority rule.
- Formation slots use the shared F23 collision-safe endpoint resolver and unique slot assignment by minimum total march distance.
- Each Major shows subordinate command links and 320/450 m command-zone rings when selected.
- Major dynamic HQ advance is applied independently to both battalions.
- Console telemetry prefixes: HQ-09F27, HQ-ORDER-09F27, HQ-SLOT-09F27, HQ-ZONE-09F27, AI-AUTHORITY-09F27.

Retained from v00.00.09f26
- AI ON moving companies may suspend movement under confirmed hostile fire, deploy Line and return fire while preserving the parent mission.
- Active bridge passage defers under-fire reaction until the bridge route clears.
- AI OFF remains direct player authority.

Retained from v00.00.09f25
- Infantry CHARGE and melee approach remain.
- Manual order followed by AI ON means local Captain authority until a NEW Major order reclaims the company.
- Command-zone principle affects command/reaction rather than weapon accuracy.
