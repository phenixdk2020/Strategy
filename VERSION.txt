v00.00.09f29w - HQ depth / rear-echelon guard
- Backup created before changes: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f29v-pre-09f29w-hq-depth.
- Adds PrototypeHqDepthGuard09F29W.
- Fixes AI-controlled Major and Regimental HQs being allowed to drift too close to or ahead of their subordinate front.
- Actual nearest active enemy direction is used as the tactical forward axis instead of relying only on the last order point.
- Major HQ minimum rear depth: 120 m behind its active company center; preferred correction depth: 175 m.
- Major HQ lateral displacement guard: 240 m from battalion centerline.
- Regimental HQ minimum rear depth: 220 m behind the midpoint of Major A / Major B; preferred correction depth: 285 m.
- Regimental HQ lateral displacement guard: 320 m from the Major midpoint centerline.
- The guard does not move HQ Transforms itself. It only corrects HqGoal/HasHqGoal and leaves physical movement to the existing F27/F28 HQ movers.
- Existing river/formation-endpoint safety is reused when selecting a corrected rear position.
- If the direct rear position is illegal, the guard tries progressively deeper and lateral safe alternatives on the same river bank.
- Manual HQ authority is preserved: Major/Regimental HQs with AI disabled are not auto-corrected.
- No company movement, combat, Square, Charge, OOB selection, tactical-map or semantic-zoom ownership is changed.
