v00.00.09m3 - Regiment-front range cone, bridge-only river crossing, ghost destination
- Selected-regiment fire fans now originate from the company line, not the mounted HQ officers.
- Detached companies cannot walk the stream; they route to the wooden bridge at z=22 and exit the far bank.
- RMB orders show a ghost rectangle on the destination footprint and a ghost path including the bridge detour.
- Fire policy is unchanged: HOLD still means no volleys. Use Ild CLOSE/MEDIUM/LONG to open fire.
- Retains 09m2 three-level command hierarchy.

Primary 09m3 QA
1. Marker: PROJECT 1864 | v00.00.09m3 TEST.
2. Select 1. Regiment companies. A range cone should sit on the soldier line, not on the three mounted officers behind them.
3. RMB across the blue stream: the ghost path should bend to the bridge. Companies must not wade the water.
4. A faint destination rectangle stays on the far bank while they march.
5. Click Ild MEDIUM or LONG; Prussian companies inside the cone should take volleys / smoke. HOLD still suppresses fire.

v00.00.09m2 - Manual / regiment officer / brigade officer command chain
- A selected Danish regiment can be steered three ways: MANUEL, REG-AI (its own officer), or BRG (7. Brigade officer commands the subordinate regiments).
- Brigade AI still issues missions only through OfficerAIController.
- The new authority layer writes those missions onto the detached 09l5 companies, so the empty regiment pivot is no longer the thing that moves.
- RMB on companies takes that regiment back to manual control.
- F11 still toggles Danish brigade AI. Prussian QA brigade remains autonomous.
- Retains 09m1 column-on-long-march and company HUD.

Primary 09m2 QA
1. Marker: PROJECT 1864 | v00.00.09m2 TEST.
2. Select 1. Regiment companies. Leave MAN selected. RMB far away: only those companies march. Regiment AI stays off.
3. Press REG-AI. The regiment officer should send all of that regiment's companies toward the nearest threat / doctrine objective without the player clicking.
4. Press BRG (or F11). Both 1. Regiment and 5. Regiment should take brigade missions; reserve stays back until the brigade commits it.
5. RMB a company while BRG is on: that regiment drops to MANUEL. The other brigade regiment keeps AI.
6. Prussian companies must also march under their officers (8th/18th). They should not freeze on the start line.
7. Long-march column policy from 09m1 still applies to AI-issued company moves.

v00.00.09m1 - Company long-march column + command HUD
- A company ordered more than about 18 m automatically changes to Column.
- It deploys back to Line about 14 m before the destination, or immediately if it enters effective range / takes fire.
- F and C still force Line / Column and lock the policy for 14 seconds.
- Selected companies get a command card (strength, formation, morale, status) and a world chip (LIN/KOL).
- Retains 09m soldier figure and company volleys.
