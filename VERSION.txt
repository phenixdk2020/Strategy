v00.00.09l2 - Company tactical control
- Companies are no longer OOB-data-only presentation blocks. Every company receives a lightweight tactical centre GameObject.
- Denmark: 2 battalions x 4 companies per regiment = 8 companies per regiment.
- Prussia: 3 battalions x 4 companies per regiment = 12 companies per regiment.
- Current four-regiment pilot therefore creates 40 company tactical centres: 16 Danish + 24 Prussian.
- Ordinary 1:1 soldiers remain GPU-instanced; there is no GameObject/MonoBehaviour/collider/NavMeshAgent/AI per ordinary soldier.
- Single LMB click on a Danish company selects that company only.
- Shift-click adds; Ctrl-click toggles.
- LMB drag box-select selects Danish company centres inside the marquee.
- RMB issues a company movement order.
- Alt+RMB appends a company waypoint.
- RMB drag defines final facing when drag distance is sufficient.
- F = Line, C = Column, H = Hold, Z/X = rotate selected company facing by 15 degrees.
- Double-clicking a company switches back to whole-regiment selection.
- Existing PlayerCommander whole-regiment control remains available and remains the path for V3 regiment movement.

v00.00.09l2 - Manual company movement ownership
- Direct manual company movement is mutually exclusive with an active parent regiment route.
- Before a company movement order is issued, the selected parent regiment PlayerCommander route is removed.
- Active parent OfficerAIController is switched OFF for a manual company order.
- Parent Regiment is set Hold so company centres do not fight against a simultaneously moving regiment transform.
- This prevents competing movement writes while preserving V3 as the authoritative whole-regiment steering layer.
- Brigade AI architecture itself is unchanged by 09l2.

v00.00.09l2 - Full-scale formation geometry fix
- The old 09k regiment-wide foot renderer created all soldiers around one Regiment pivot and produced long snake/L-shaped formations at full manpower.
- 09l2 anchors 1:1 infantry to individual company tactical centres instead.
- Company Line = 3 ranks with approximately 0.52 m file spacing and 0.76 m rank spacing.
- Company Column = 8 files across with compact march depth.
- Four companies form a battalion line; battalions occupy separate depth rows inside the regiment template.
- The old 09k ordinary-infantry renderer is disabled only after its retained mounted Regimental HQ groups have been created.
- Mounted Regimental HQ, Brigade HQ, dual standards, cavalry and artillery remain active.
- 09l2 company renderer follows terrain height for each instanced soldier.

v00.00.09l2 - Company casualty state
- Regiment.CurrentStrength remains authoritative for the existing regiment-level combat resolver in this gate.
- Strength changes are reconciled into company CurrentStrength values after reserving regimental staff manpower.
- Company losses are distributed across company states rather than reducing only one visual tail.
- Company slot order is deterministically shuffled for rendering, so casualties appear as distributed gaps rather than cutting the end off a formation and creating an artificial L shape.
- A later combat gate will move fire eligibility, ammo, LOS/exposure and casualty targeting fully to company level.

v00.00.09l2 - Selection modes
- Company selection and Regiment selection are separate command modes.
- Company selection clears PlayerCommander regiment selection.
- Whole-regiment selection clears company selection.
- The legacy 09h regiment-centre box selector is disabled once 09l2 company control installs; 09l2 owns the marquee and selects companies.
- Enemy/Prussian companies are not directly selectable by the player.

Retained v00.00.09l / 09l1
- Danish side remains the historical working pilot 7. Brigade: 1. Infanteri-Regiment (~1540) + 11. Infanteri-Regiment (~1584).
- Prussian 8th + 18th pair remains explicitly QA until a date-specific historical brigade OOB is sourced.
- One Brigade HQ per side remains active; each Brigade HQ has six mounted staff figures.
- Regimental mounted HQ groups remain three riders each.
- Brigade missions remain Hold / DefendArea / AttackCaptureArea.
- Regiment brigade roles remain Reserve / Support / Engaged / Manoeuvre / Withdraw.
- Danish Brigade AI starts OFF and F11 toggles it; Prussian QA Brigade AI starts ON.
- Dual standards remain national/army flag + regimental identity flag.
- Danish field battery remains 8 guns / approximately 190 personnel.
- Danish cavalry pilot remains personnel/horses/mounted-effective separated.
- 09l1 Unity 6.6 compatibility fixes remain retained.

Retained movement/combat baseline
- Navigation V3 remains whole-regiment steering authority.
- 09i1 Farmhouse/Barn continuation fix remains active.
- 09h4 manual AI-OFF regiment route recovery remains active.
- Decorative trees/fence posts remain soft/pass-through; Farmhouse/Barn remain hard; river remains bridge-only.
- Weapon-specific reload timing/visual state remains based on actual outgoing volley nextFireTime.
- Existing Regiment fire policy, range cone, accuracy, morale/cohesion and combat resolver are unchanged in 09l2.

Known transition constraints
- Company movement in 09l2 is a tactical child-centre model under the Regiment command entity. Independent company obstacle/path navigation is a later gate so V3 is not replaced simultaneously with company-control introduction.
- Whole-regiment movement still uses the central tactical collider/bounds of the existing map.
- Unity Input Manager deprecation warning remains non-blocking; Input System migration is a separate controls revision.
