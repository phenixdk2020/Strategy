v00.00.09l5 - Independent company movement authority
- Root cause from the 09l4 video: company tactical centres were still parented under Regiment.transform, so parent movement/rotation physically dragged all companies as one rigid body.
- Active company tactical centres are now detached into an independent world-space tactical root while retaining their OOB ParentRegiment relationship in data.
- Regimental HQ remains the command parent, but it is no longer the transform parent of the company formations.
- Parent OfficerAI movement is suppressed in this focused company-control QA gate and the parent Regiment pivot is held in place.
- Whole-regiment selection is converted to selecting all subordinate Danish companies, so a regiment order becomes a group of company orders instead of moving one giant pivot.

v00.00.09l5 - Group order geometry fix
- 09l2 multi-company RMB orders previously sorted every selected company laterally and rebuilt the selection as one giant continuous line.
- 09l5 overrides that group destination in the same mouse-up frame.
- Multi-company orders now preserve the selected companies' relative world-space offsets around their group centroid.
- Normal RMB translates the company group while retaining its internal spacing and each company's existing facing.
- RMB drag may rotate the relative offsets as a group around the order anchor and set a common explicit facing.
- Each company still receives its own independent destination and moves through its own tactical centre.
- Single-company RMB orders remain unchanged.
- Console marker: COMPANY-AUTH-09L5|Installed=True|TransformParent=IndependentWorldSpace|GroupOrders=PreserveRelativeCompanyOffsets.

Retained v00.00.09l4 - Company selection / combat authority / guidons
- 1:1 ordinary soldiers remain GPU-instanced and have no individual colliders.
- Screen-space company hit testing selects the visible company footprint rather than only HQ/collider geometry.
- Active company-parent regiments remain forced to HoldFire in this QA gate; mounted HQ does not fire the regiment volley.
- Parent regiment range cones remain hidden while company combat is not yet implemented.
- Full historical national/regimental standards remain at Regimental HQ.
- Every active company retains two smaller tactical guidons: national identity + regiment/company identity.
