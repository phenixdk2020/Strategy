V10N5 PURPOSE:
- Add a controlled withdrawal order with a player-selected rally point.
- Units not in combat contact march directly toward the rally point.
- Units in combat contact use a fighting-withdrawal cycle: back away while facing the enemy, halt briefly to fire, then continue withdrawing until clear.
- Add BRYD KONTAKT as a separate emergency disengagement order.
- Keep uncontrolled morale rout/flight separate from player-ordered withdrawal.

TACTICAL COMMANDS:
1. TILBAGETRÆK HERTIL
- Select one or more Danish regiments.
- Press the TILBAGETRÆK HERTIL control.
- Click a terrain point to define a common rally point.
- Units outside combat contact enter RallyMarch and move directly to the rally point.
- Units inside combat contact enter FightingWithdrawal first.
- FightingWithdrawal alternates short backward movement phases and short firing/holding phases.
- During fighting withdrawal the regiment keeps its frontage toward the nearest threat instead of turning its back.
- Once safely outside mutual effective engagement distance for a confirmation interval, the unit switches to RallyMarch.

2. BRYD KONTAKT
- Applies immediately to the currently selected Danish regiments.
- Fire policy is temporarily forced to HOLD while disengaging.
- Unit turns away from the nearest threat and uses an emergency speed boost to leave engagement range.
- If the unit already has a rally point, it continues toward that rally point after contact is broken.
- If no rally point exists, it halts after safely disengaging.
- Original fire policy is restored after disengagement.

3. ROUT / FLUGT REMAINS SEPARATE
- Existing Regiment.IsRouted and morale-triggered Route() remain the uncontrolled rout state.
- FightingWithdrawal and BreakContact are controlled orders and do not mark the unit routed.

OFFICER AI INTERACTION:
- A withdrawal order temporarily takes direct control from OfficerAIController if AI UNIT is enabled.
- Officer AI is restored after the controlled withdrawal order completes, provided the regiment has not routed.
- This is a prototype bridge until the later HQ/order-delay system represents withdrawal as a native officer mission.

UI:

- TILBAGETRÆK HERTIL arms the next terrain click as rally-point selection.
- BRYD KONTAKT issues immediate emergency disengagement.
- Status text shows armed target count / active withdrawals.
