v00.00.09f5 - Company-scale baseline
- Current 1v1 test units now represent companies rather than full regiments.
- Active Danish and Prussian test companies are normalised to 190 men.
- Internal Regiment class/names are retained for compatibility, while UI identifies the units as companies.
- At F9 living scale 1:1, one actual living man corresponds to one rendered soldier.
- At F9 fallen scale 1:1, one actual loss corresponds to one persistent fallen visual.
- PrototypeCompanyScale09F5 owns the 190-man TEST normalisation.

v00.00.09f5 - 1851 three-rank line
- Danish 1851 line infantry uses 3 ranks as the prototype baseline.
- A full 190-man company therefore forms approximately 64 + 63 + 63 men.
- Lateral spacing is approximately 0.75 m per man, giving a full-strength line frontage of approximately 48 m.
- Two-rank line is not hardcoded as the 1851 default; it is documented as a later doctrine/regulation research and adoption option.
- PrototypeBattleVisuals09F5 owns the three-rank 1:1 visual formation.

v00.00.09f5 - Battlefield metre scale and footprint
- Tactical design assumption is 1 Unity world unit = 1 metre.
- Soldier size, rank spacing, line frontage, march-column depth, collider, selected outline and range fans use the same metre-scale assumption.
- PrototypeUnitFootprint09F5 derives BoxCollider dimensions from current strength and current formation.
- The old circular selection disc is hidden.
- Selected units receive a rectangular outline matching the real Line or Column footprint.
- Close/Medium/Long range fans originate from the current formation frontage instead of the old fixed muzzle width.

v00.00.09f5 - Weapon and range visual
- Danish soldiers retain the richer dark-blue/light-blue 1850s prototype uniform.
- Musket/rifle is made visually clearer at tactical camera distance.
- A fixed socket bayonet is visible on the weapon.
- Current TEST range values are Close 100 m, Effective/Medium 200 m and Long/Maximum 400 m.
- These are current prototype tuning values, not the final historical weapon database.
- Final per-weapon ranges remain data-driven research work.

v00.00.09f5 - Hover UI and text cleanup
- Permanent floating TextMesh labels over formations are suppressed; the physical flags remain.
- Hovering a unit shows a compact information box with unit name, nation, strength, fallen/losses, formation, current order/AI state, morale, cohesion, ammunition and weapon/range data.
- Danish display name is 1. Kompagni - Sjællandske Livregiment.
- Prussian display name is 8. Kompagni - Preussen.
- Battle result logic now supports the 1v1 scenario rather than requiring four active regiments.

v00.00.09f5 - Time controls
- Battle clock and simulation speed controls are reduced in size.
- Time controls are moved to the upper-right corner.
- Existing hotkeys and simulation speeds are retained.
