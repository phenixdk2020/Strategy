v00.00.09f16 - Major selection and hover
- Major is now handled as a first-class selectable HQ through PrototypeMajorSelectionBridge09F16.
- Normal left-click selection flow is preserved: PlayerCommander resolves Regiment selection first; the Major bridge resolves HQ selection immediately afterwards.
- Selecting Major shows the HQ selection ring and command-links; clicking another unit/ground removes Major selection.
- Major gets mouse-over tooltip with rank/echelon, HQ AI state, horses, subordinate count and current order.

v00.00.09f16 - Major AI toggle
- Major HQ still starts AI OFF.
- HQ panel now has a working HQ AI ON/OFF button.
- PrototypeMajorHQ09F16 provides a first autonomous battalion-AI MVP: periodically evaluate nearest enemy + battalion strength/cohesion and choose HOLD/DEFEND/ADVANCE/ATTACK.
- This is a temporary behavior baseline; final officer stats/personality/order-compliance logic remains later work.

v00.00.09f16 - Shared compact bottom UI
- Major and company panels now use the same dark olive/brass PrototypeUiTheme09F15 visual language.
- Both panels use the same centered bottom footprint: about 940 x 104 px maximum.
- Major panel keeps the six HQ orders but is substantially lower than 09f15.
- Company panel is restyled to the same panel/header/button theme and retains AI, doctrine, facing, fire policy, F9 and aggression controls.

v00.00.09f16 - Straight battalion DEFEND front
- FORSVAR HER now computes one common battalion facing from the objective toward the nearest relevant enemy.
- Both subordinate companies receive slots on one common line perpendicular to that facing.
- Normal spacing increased to 60 m so ~48 m company fronts do not overlap.
- On final arrival each company snaps the final <=3.5 m to its exact slot, goes to Line, Holds and receives the exact same final facing.
- The old per-company nearest-enemy final facing is removed from this completion path because it produced the skewed two-company front seen in 09f15 QA.
