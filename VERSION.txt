v00.00.09j - Fire & Reload Animation Pass
- New PrototypeReloadAnimation09J is presentation-only and runs after the 09i articulated soldier pass.
- It detects an ACTUAL outgoing volley by observing Regiment.nextFireTime move to a new future timestamp. This fixes the previous visual shortcut where 09i used HasHitFeedback, which means the regiment had been hit rather than that it had fired.
- Actual volley -> short shoulder/fire pose -> weapon-specific reload animation -> ready pose.
- Danish Rifled Muzzle-Loader representatives use a longer approximate lower/vertical rifle, cartridge/charge, ramrod/loading and recover sequence.
- Prussian Dreyse representatives use a shorter lower-rifle, bolt/cartridge, close/recover sequence.
- Reload animation duration follows the existing real combat reload window, including weapon profile, experience multiplier and the existing volley randomisation. 09j does not set or modify nextFireTime.
- A small deterministic per-soldier stagger prevents perfect clone motion while keeping the regiment visually coordinated.
- Standard-bearers without an active rifle are excluded from rifle reload posing.
- 09j takes presentation ownership of riflemen arms/rifle only after 09i has run: fire/reload/march/ready arms are therefore based on correct outgoing-fire state while 09i continues to own legs, body, LOD, uniforms and equipment visuals.
- Expected telemetry: RELOAD-09J|Installed=True, VolleyDetected=True, ReloadAnimation=True and ReloadComplete=True.
- No movement, destination, formation, accuracy, hit, ammo, morale, cohesion, Officer AI or PlayerCommander logic is changed.
- Design supplement: docs/parts/part-09j-fire-reload-animation.md.
