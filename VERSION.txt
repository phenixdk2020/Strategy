v00.00.09f23 - Manual authority ordering fix
- Direct player company orders now release Major authority BEFORE PlayerCommander consumes the same input frame.
- PrototypeManualOrderAuthority execution order changed from +250 to -1200.
- Selected companies are detached from the live Major mission, HQ mission visuals are cleared, and Officer AI is OFF before RMB/key orders are interpreted.
- This fixes the case where a company received a manual order but continued following the Major mission because the input had already been processed while AI authority was still active.
- Major AI cannot reclaim an AI-OFF manually detached company until a NEW Major order or explicit company AI ON.
- Console telemetry: AI-AUTHORITY-09F23.

v00.00.09f23 - Shared collision-safe formation slot resolver
- Major formations and manual multi-company Line orders now share PrototypeFormationSlotSafety09F23.
- A final slot is illegal if the company footprint overlaps Farmhouse/Barn, open river water, battlefield bounds or another reserved company slot.
- Accepted company slots are reserved immediately with minimum centre separation 55 m.
- If a slot is blocked, the resolver searches along the requested formation line first and only uses a shallow fore/aft stagger as a last resort.
- This prevents two independently corrected companies from being pushed onto the same building edge.

v00.00.09f23 - Manual drawn Line safety
- Manual multi-company Line slots are generated from the player-drawn line, corrected for buildings and reserved against one another.
- Final legal slots are assigned to selected companies by minimum total squared march distance across all 2-4 company permutations.
- This fixes one company standing off the intended line while another ends on/in an object.
- Manual group ground/enemy raycasts use the expanded 5000 m interaction distance.
- Console telemetry: GROUP-LINE-09F23.

v00.00.09f23 - Major slot deconfliction
- A final Major formation pass runs after f22 objective-centred placement.
- Front slots are regenerated from the commander objective, corrected as one reserved set and assigned by shortest total march distance.
- Reserve is included in the same final footprint reservation system and cannot collapse onto a front company.
- Individual facing toward known enemy remains retained where available.
- AI-OFF/manual companies are explicitly excluded so this layer cannot steal player authority.
- Console telemetry: HQ-SLOTS-09F23.
