v00.00.09m - Strategy-Kamp testable battle
- Ordinary company infantry is no longer the 09l2 capsule/sphere stand-in.
- New GPU-instanced 1864 figure: coat, skirt, belt, trousers, boots, arms, hands, head, kepi or pickelhaube, pack, cross-straps, cartridge box, rifle and bayonet.
- Danish kepi + visor + red band; Prussian pickelhaube dome + spike + front plate.
- March, fire and reload poses remain presentation-only.
- Limited fallen figures appear in emptied casualty slots.
- Parent HQ remains HoldFire. Companies now fire from their own tactical centres.
- Company volleys use the documented 09l5 expected-hit formula and apply hits through Regiment.ReceiveVolley so company strengths thin via the existing 09l2 reconciler.
- Standing companies turn toward a target in arc before firing. Muzzle smoke emits from the company frontage.
- Console markers: KAMP-VISUAL-09M and KAMP-FIRE-09M.

Retained v00.00.09l5 - Independent company movement authority
- Active company tactical centres remain detached into an independent world-space tactical root.
- Multi-company RMB orders preserve relative world-space offsets around the group centroid.

Retained v00.00.09l4 - Company selection / combat authority / guidons
- 1:1 ordinary soldiers remain GPU-instanced and have no individual colliders.
- Screen-space company hit testing selects the visible company footprint.
- Full historical national/regimental standards remain at Regimental HQ.
- Every active company retains two smaller tactical guidons.

Retained v00.00.09l3 - Reduced QA battle
- Active infantry remains two regiments while company combat is proven.
- Denmark: 1. Infanteri-Regiment, approximately 1,540 men, 8 companies.
- Prussia: 8th Regiment (QA), approximately 2,460 men, 12 companies.

Primary 09m QA
1. Unity 6000.6.0f1 compiles without red errors.
2. Build marker: PROJECT 1864 | v00.00.09m TEST.
3. KAMP-VISUAL-09M reports Figure=1864Infantry and VisiblePersonnel around 4,000.
4. Danish infantry read as dark-blue coats, grey trousers and kepi. Prussian infantry read as dark coats and spiked helmets.
5. Zooming to a company shows belt, pack, straps, rifle wood/iron and bayonet rather than a single capsule.
6. KAMP-FIRE-09M reports Owner=CompanyCentre.
7. March Danish companies into Prussian fire arc. Both sides produce muzzle smoke and company ranks thin.
8. Parent HQ remains HoldFire and does not fire a giant regiment volley from the mounted staff.
9. Moving a company still moves only that company. Group RMB still preserves spacing.
10. Fallen figures appear in thinned companies without collapsing the formation into an L-shape.
