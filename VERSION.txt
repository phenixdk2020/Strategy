v00.00.09f29e - Regimental defense + objective visuals + coordinated attack
- Adds a compact Oberstløjtnant / Regimental HQ HUD using the same F29C red/green visual language as Major and Company control.
- Regimental HUD is divided into ENHEDSINFO / AI, REGIMENTSORDRER and BATALJONER UNDER OBERSTLØJTNANT.
- Regimental HUD shows total men, losses, morale, cohesion and average ammunition plus status rows for both subordinate battalions.
- AI ON is green / AI OFF red; DEF/BAL/OFF are red when inactive and green when active.
- Regimental order buttons are ANGRIB HER, FORSVAR HER, RYK FREM, TILBAGETRÆK, SAML and STOP/HOLD.
- The legacy F28 HUD remains as a logic dependency but the new F29E HUD draws over it and consumes the existing 90 px command-panel pointer area so hidden controls cannot double-fire.
- A committed Regimental objective now remains visible while the Oberstløjtnant is selected. The marker is a terrain-following order-coloured circle plus centre cross and screen-space order label.
- First persistent objective radii: DEFEND 72 m, ATTACK 58 m, ASSEMBLE 40 m and generic advance/withdraw 50 m.
- Selecting the Oberstløjtnant now exposes the complete command chain: Oberstløjtnant -> both Majors -> all eight companies.
- Selecting the Oberstløjtnant also exposes all company mission destination footprints and active routes, making the decomposition from regimental objective to company slots visible without selecting each Major.
- Precise slot-arrival hardening corrects the old 4.5 m mission-arrival window. If F27 marks a company arrived while its centre is still outside the displayed footprint, F29E completes only the final metres and accepts approximately 0.85 m from the slot centre.
- Precise arrival does not override F26 under-fire reaction and does not teleport units.
- Regimental FORSVAR HER now anchors a continuous defensive frontage around the clicked point. The two battalion centres use approximately +/-84 m lateral separation instead of the generic +/-170 m split, so the six-company front produced by two local battalion reserves meets through the ordered centre instead of leaving an open corridor.
- Regimental AttackHere front/reserve and front/flank roles are now actually corrected in runtime: the battalion nearest the objective is FRONT and the other becomes RESERVE or FLANK. The original F28 total-travel permutation no longer determines those tactical roles.
- Adds coordinated explicit AttackTarget deployment for multiple companies attacking the same enemy. Companies receive distinct formation-safe attack slots instead of independently converging on the target centre.
- Coordinated attack keeps OfficerAIController as the physical movement owner, yields to a disabled controller owned by Major/higher command, and suspends itself during F26 under-fire reaction.
- For 2-4 attackers the first implementation deploys companies on a frontal arc around the target while preserving left/right ordering to reduce crossing; larger groups put additional companies in support positions.
- Coordinated attack slots are resolved through F23 formation-footprint safety with current PrototypeBootstrap battlefield bounds; the obsolete V1 static small-map destination clamp is no longer used for physical coordinated attack movement.
- At coordinated slots, companies HOLD the tactical position, face the designated target and use the normal fire system; material slot movement can trigger replanning while small changes are ignored.
- Adds PrototypeWideBattlefieldNavigation09F29E for the 5760 x 3840 m battlefield. The old V4/V3/V1 navigation writers and old recovery layer are disabled because their active V4 grid was still bounded to approximately +/-176 x +/-116 m.
- Wide-map navigation uses PrototypeBootstrap battlefield bounds, preserves persistent final goals and uses lightweight local obstacle detours instead of clamping legal units back to the old small prototype area.
- PrototypeRiverBridgeOnly09F3 remains the sole river-crossing authority; wide navigation does not reinterpret bridge staging/entry/cross/exit steering as new final goals.
- Retains F29D semantic zoom/NATO overlays, F29C Major/Company HUD, F29B attack-authority protection and F29A hierarchy/range visual fixes.
- New telemetry prefixes: HUD-09F29E, REG-HARDEN-09F29E, REG-DEFEND-09F29E, REG-ATTACK-09F29E, HQ-ARRIVAL-09F29E, COORD-ATTACK-09F29E and NAV-WIDE-09F29E.
- Backup before this revision: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f29d-pre-09f29e-regimental-visual-defense.

v00.00.09f29d - Semantic zoom + NATO tactical overlay
- Adds a runtime semantic zoom manager with four visual levels: CLOSE, MEDIUM, OPERATIONAL and STRATEGIC.
- HQ visibility is prioritized: Major/Battalion and Regimental HQ receive fixed-screen-size tactical counters from moderate zoom, before generic company counters appear.
- Major/Battalion counters use NATO echelon II and Regimental/Oberstløjtnant uses III; company-scale infantry uses I.
- Operational zoom overlays all company-scale formations on both sides with NATO-style infantry counters, facing arrows, strength bars, current/initial men and morale; Danish counters also show ammunition.
- Friendly counters are blue, enemy red, selected units/HQ gold and routed units grey.
- Strategic zoom hides company/HQ MeshRenderers and ParticleSystemRenderers while leaving terrain, simulation, colliders, AI, LineRenderers, command links and order state active.
- Zooming back in restores the hidden 3D meshes.
- HQ counters include a screen-space beacon/stem down to the real world position so commanders remain locatable over terrain and units.
- Current QA thresholds use camera height: HQ markers ~72 m, Medium ~135 m, Operational ~235 m, Strategic ~390 m.
- Adds a top-right tactical-view indicator while not in Close view.
- Telemetry prefix: SEMANTIC-ZOOM-09F29D.
- Backup before this revision: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f29c-pre-09f29d-semantic-zoom.

v00.00.09f29c - Unified command HUD + company naming
- Adds one compact 90 px tactical command HUD for Major/Battalion and Company/Kaptajn control.
- Major HUD contains ENHEDSINFO / AI, BATALJONSORDRER and KOMPAGNIER UNDER MAJOR.
- Company HUD contains ENHEDSINFO, AI/DOKTRIN, SKYDNING, ORDRER/BEVÆGELSE and FORMATION.
- AI and active state buttons use green-active / red-inactive coding.
- Company fire controls: HOLD/CLOSE/MED/LONG.
- Fighting withdrawal controls: -> MED, -> LONG and -> UD.
- Company movement controls: TVANG, CHARGE and STOP.
- Formation controls: LINJE, KOLONNE and SQUARE using the existing F29 square implementation.
- Danish display naming is normalized to 1. KOMPAGNI through 8. KOMPAGNI while legacy internal prototype identifiers remain for compatibility.
- Backup before this revision: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f29b-pre-09f29c-hud.

v00.00.09f29b - Battlefield scale + attack AI hardening
- Tactical battlefield is doubled in both linear dimensions from 2880 x 1920 m to 5760 x 3840 m.
- Road, river and vegetation are generated across the complete enlarged battlefield.
- Two Prussian company-scale QA formations are retained for target-selection and multi-target testing.
- Attack frontage planning no longer writes competing OrderMove/OrderHold commands every think cycle.
- Frontage and approach layers respect disabled OfficerAIController state and F26 under-fire reaction.
- Explicit AttackTarget is sticky and no longer replaced by whichever enemy happens to be nearest.
- AttackNearest gains target hysteresis and locks to explicit AttackTarget after entering the engagement envelope.
- Retains F29A terrain-following hierarchy links, officer target cursor and full company-footprint river endpoint protection.

v00.00.09f29 - Infantry square / cavalry-threat foundation
- Adds manual/AI SQUARE state layered over the Line/Column movement kernel.
- Q toggles selected Danish companies into square; F reforms to Line and C to Column.
- Square formation time depends on cohesion and cannot fire while still forming.
- Ready square uses multi-side range presentation and reduced firing effectiveness so it is not a 360-degree full-company volley.
- F29 exposes the mounted-threat API used by future cavalry.
- AI ON infantry may auto-square against a sufficiently strong mounted threat.
- AI OFF remains literal player authority.
