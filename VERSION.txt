v00.00.09f6 - Selection visual correction
- Selected-unit outline is now terrain-following along all four edges so sloped ground cannot hide portions of the box.
- Selection footprint is derived from the actual current Line/Column footprint.
- A small yellow pulsing selection orb is restored above the selected unit centre.
- PrototypeTacticalVisuals09F6 disables the older 09f5 selection-outline authority.

v00.00.09f6 - Fire cone restored
- Close/Medium/Long range fans are explicitly rebuilt after the older visual layers.
- Fire fans start across the full current formation frontage instead of a fixed central muzzle width.
- Fire sector remains 120 degrees total (plus/minus 60 degrees).
- Current TEST distances remain Close 100 m, Medium/Effective 200 m, Long/Maximum 400 m at 1 Unity unit = 1 metre.
- Fans follow terrain height and are made more visible at tactical zoom.

v00.00.09f6 - Destination footprint correction
- The old fixed 19 x 5.2 m route destination box is no longer authoritative.
- Permanent route destination ghosts are resized from the company's actual current strength and requested final formation.
- Live RMB destination preview is also overridden to the same dynamic footprint for a single selected company.
- A full 190-man, three-rank Line therefore previews a frontage of approximately 48 m plus a small UI margin.

v00.00.09f6 - Automatic march column restored
- The old AI-only approach-formation policy is runtime-disabled by PrototypeMarchColumn09F6.
- Player-routed and AI-routed units now share one formation policy.
- Long movement (steering destination about 28 m or farther) automatically changes to Column.
- Units deploy back to Line when enemy contact closes to about 70 m or when the final movement stops.
- Temporary bridge-entry / bridge-exit steering destinations do not force premature Line deployment.
- PrototypeRiverBridgeOnly09F3 remains sole owner of bridge-only river routing; 09f6 writes formations only.

v00.00.09f5 - Company-scale baseline retained
- Current 1v1 test units represent companies rather than full regiments.
- Active Danish and Prussian test companies are normalised to 190 men.
- Internal Regiment class/names are retained for compatibility, while UI identifies the units as companies.
- At F9 living scale 1:1, one actual living man corresponds to one rendered soldier.
- At F9 fallen scale 1:1, one actual loss corresponds to one persistent fallen visual.

v00.00.09f5 - 1851 three-rank line retained
- Danish 1851 line infantry uses 3 ranks as the prototype baseline.
- A full 190-man company therefore forms approximately 64 + 63 + 63 men.
- Lateral spacing is approximately 0.75 m per man, giving a full-strength line frontage of approximately 48 m.
- Two-rank line is documented as a later doctrine/regulation research and adoption option.

v00.00.09f5 - Weapon / UI retained
- Danish soldiers retain the richer dark-blue/light-blue 1850s prototype uniform.
- Musket/rifle and fixed bayonet remain visible at tactical zoom.
- Permanent floating TextMesh labels over formations remain suppressed; physical flags remain.
- Hovering a unit shows unit name, nation, strength, fallen/losses, formation, order/AI state, morale, cohesion, ammunition and weapon/range data.
- Battle clock and simulation speed controls remain compact in the upper-right corner.
