- Preserve all v10m city population/tier/build restrictions and movement routing.
- Keep historical accuracy explicit: v10n temporary polygon geometry must never be treated as historical facit.

ZONE OVERLAY:
- New CampaignZoneOverlayV010N auto-loads after the campaign scene.
- All 20 ZONE-REG-01 identities receive a visible closed boundary polygon.
- Z toggles the complete zone overlay ON/OFF.
- Overlay includes an on-screen v00.00.10n status badge.
- Prototype sectors are generated from canonical zone centres inside five geographic groups: Jylland, Fyn/Langeland, Sjælland/Møn, Lolland-Falster and Bornholm.
- Geometry metadata explicitly sets HistoricallyExactGeometry=False.
