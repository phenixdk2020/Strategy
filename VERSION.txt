V10N6E COUNTY / AMT GEOMETRY:
- The sparse city+zone-centre-only partition is no longer the preferred geometry source.
- CampaignZoneOverlayV010N downloads/caches Data/Geo.csv from christianvedels/A_perfect_storm.
- That replication dataset documents its parish geography as originating from DigDag and representing Danish parishes on 1 January 1820.
- Runtime groups parish centroids by historical County + Hundred and creates one averaged historical seed per Herred.
- Canonical CITY-REG-01 and ZONE-REG-01 guard sites are added after the historical seed field.
- The resulting geometry remains clipped to the existing Denmark land scaffold and remains overlap-free by Voronoi construction.
- Geometry mode: DIGDAG_1820_HERRED_SEEDS_LAND_CLIPPED_APPROX.
- The historical CSV is cached in Application.persistentDataPath after first successful download.
- If historical seed loading fails, the previous canonical city+zone-centre seed model is used as an explicit fallback and a warning is logged.

- HistoricallyExactGeometry therefore remains FALSE.
