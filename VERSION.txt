v00.00.09f28 - Physical Regimental HQ / Oberstløjtnant
- Adds one physical selectable Regimental HQ commanded by Oberstløjtnant.
- Canonical runtime chain is now: Oberstløjtnant -> Major A/Major B -> 8 company-scale infantry units.
- Regimental HQ has 4 horses and its own full-width 90 px bottom HUD.
- Regimental HQ exposes AI ON/OFF, DEF/BAL/OFF doctrine and the same six mission verbs: ANGRIB HER / FORSVAR HER / RYK FREM / TILBAGETRÆK / SAML / HOLD.
- Regimental orders are NOT sent directly to companies. F28 assigns battalion objectives to the two Majors; the shared F27 Major core translates those objectives into company missions.
- New Regimental order reclaims both Majors into higher-command authority by setting their battalion AI ON through the F27 shared core.
- Battalion objectives are assigned to nearest Major to reduce route crossing.
- First regiment dispositions: 2 abreast, 1 forward + 1 reserve, or 1 forward + 1 flank depending on mission, doctrine and known enemy count.
- Offensive doctrine may select a battalion flank when the known enemy picture is limited; Defensive/Balanced may preserve a regimental reserve.
- Regimental AI first-pass: with AI ON and no explicit mission, OFF doctrine may attack nearest known enemy; otherwise it establishes a defensive mission around regiment centre.
- Explicit player regimental mission remains commander intent and is not churned every AI think cycle.
- Regimental HQ dynamically follows the two Majors in bounds when AI is ON, normally around 360 m behind their midpoint/front direction.
- Regimental command visual bands: IN COMMAND <=800 m, EXTENDED <=1100 m, OUT OF COMMAND >1100 m. First F28 pass visualises these bands; courier/delivery delay remains later B-288 work.
- Selecting Regimental HQ shows command links to the two Majors; selecting a Major continues to show only its four company links.
- Console telemetry prefixes: REG-HQ-09F28, REG-ORDER-09F28, REG-AI-09F28, REG-AUTHORITY-09F28.
