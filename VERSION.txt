- Infantry deploy threshold is now nearest enemy MaximumRange plus a 35 m safety/reform buffer.

- FIX: cavalry bridge routing is now split into planned route and actual narrow bridge approach.

- CAV travels toward the bridge in its normal Line or normal four-abreast Column.
- Two-abreast bridge/defile geometry activates only within approximately 36 m of the near bridge bank.

- The late enemy QA overlay creates missing Close/Medium/Long fan renderers as soon as a Prussian Regiment exists, so cones can be visible from initial battle setup rather than waiting for another visual/selection state to create them.
- Enemy cones remain always-visible TEST/QA visuals and do not grant LOS/target knowledge/fire authority.
- Existing F30W same-bank routing/OOB AI, F30V final-slot arrival and earlier command/CAV fixes remain inherited.
