V10N6G AMT GEOMETRY:
- n6f's grid/raster county contour is retired and must not be used as the visible Amt layer.
- CampaignHistoricalAmtPolygonsV010N6G downloads/caches the actual DigDag-derived parish SHP + DBF from christianvedels/A_perfect_storm.
- The source repository documents these parish polygons as Danish parish geography valid 1 January 1820 and originally from DigDag.
- n6g parses the SHP polygon records and DBF AMT/SOGN/HERRED attributes directly in Unity.
- Only polygon edges shared by parishes belonging to different Amts are rendered.
- Coastlines and internal parish edges inside the same Amt are not rendered as Amt borders.
- Shared boundary segments are chained into smooth LineRenderer polylines; there is no grid staircase and no Voronoi ownership geometry.
- Geometry mode: DIGDAG_1820_SOURCE_PARISH_POLYGON_EDGES.
- n6f grid root, n6e polygon lines and n5 polish lines are suppressed when n6g source geometry is ready.

V10N6G CLICK OWNERSHIP:
- Direct city click always uses canonical City.ZoneId.
- Ordinary land click uses point-in-polygon against the same source parish polygons that drive the visible Amt boundaries.
- No nearest-zone-centre resolver, Voronoi resolver or grid ownership field is used.
- Open sea/uncovered space returns no Amt.
- CampaignZoneInfoPanelV010N6G disables the older n6f/n3 info panels.

HISTORICAL GUARDRAIL:
- The source geometry is 1 January 1820 parish geography, not an exact 1 January 1851 Amt/Region export.
- HistoricallyExact1851 remains FALSE.
- Exact dated DigDag Amt/Region polygons for the campaign date remain the later production target.
- n6g is nevertheless source-polygon geometry, not a synthetic centre/grid approximation.

V10N6G CITY ART:

- Old CITY_ICON_/CITY_ART_ children are removed before n6g installation.
