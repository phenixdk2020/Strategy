V10N3 PURPOSE:
- Make visible zone borders thinner, sharper and less fuzzy.
- Remove exact duplicate/shared border rendering.
- Stop drawing the yellow zone overlay along the outer coastline envelope in normal view.
- Preserve the v10n2 land-clipped non-overlap prototype as the geometry source.
- Add a small contextual zone information card in the upper-left corner on map selection.

ZONE LINE POLISH:
- New CampaignZoneLinePolishV010N3 runs after the v10n2 overlay is built.
- Reads the v10n2 polygon loops rather than replacing the zone data model.
- Snaps visible endpoints to a small common world grid.
- Canonicalises segment direction and deduplicates identical segments.
- Renders unique internal segments with a thinner opaque line.
- Removes cap/corner smoothing from the polished render layer.
- Detects segments aligned with the current DNK_Coast_* envelope and omits them from the yellow zone overlay.
- The basemap/coastline therefore defines the coastal edge instead of a second yellow outline.
- Original v10n2 polygon LineRenderers remain as prototype geometry but are disabled after the n3 polished layer is built.
- Z continues to toggle the complete overlay hierarchy, including the n3 polished child layer.

COMPACT ZONE INFO:
- New CampaignZoneInfoPanelV010N3 appears after clicking a zone marker, city marker, or Danish land area.
- Danish land-area clicks resolve the nearest ZONE-REG-01 centre using the same current prototype partition principle.
- Compact card shows ZoneId, zone name, number of cities, A/B/C distribution, summed 1850 urban population, city names, land neighbours and ferry neighbours.
- Direct city clicks also show selected city, tier, population and heavy military construction rule.
- The compact card has an X close button.
- A map click restores the HUD if minimal HUD was active, because explicit selection should reveal context.
- During normal map selection, the old large INFO/F2 panel is closed automatically.
- INFO/F2 can still reopen the legacy detailed panel, which replaces rather than overlaps the compact card.
