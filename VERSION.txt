v00.00.09f30n - Regiment HUD Parity + Cavalry Screen/Opportunity AI + Anti-Cavalry Infantry Reaction
- Backup before F30N: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f30m-pre-09f30n-hud-cav-contact at F30M HEAD c27aa704cfe7c1316206871f075034163fb54f1c.
- FIX: Brigade/Division HUD now uses the Regimental HQ HUD as the literal visual template: identical panel/header theme, header height, AI/doctrine placement, info geometry, six-button single-row mission layout, spacing and button palette.
- Higher-HQ active mission state is shown in captions instead of introducing a separate blue/green/red HUD colour language.
- Cavalry attack AI now uses SCREEN -> OPPORTUNITY -> CHARGE rather than geometry-only charge commitment.
- Cavalry waits at flank/rear stand-off distance when the target is not yet fixed or weakened.
- A charge opportunity opens when friendly Danish infantry has the target in local fire contact, or when target morale/cohesion has materially degraded.
- Cavalry route planning now treats hostile Prussian companies as avoidance bubbles and inserts detour waypoints instead of riding through enemy infantry to reach a flank point.
- Detour waypoints are intermediate only; CAV resumes toward its intended screen/flank point after clearing the obstacle.
- Higher-HQ attack staging also uses enemy-bubble avoidance.
- Prussian infantry can now acquire Danish cavalry inside its active fire-policy range and fire normal musket volleys using the existing reload, range, accuracy, smoke and cohesion pipeline.
- Infantry volleys now apply casualties, morale/cohesion shock and possible FALTER response to cavalry; 1:1 mounted/dismounted CAV visuals hide casualties with CurrentStrength.
- Infantry mounted-threat / Square assessment is now team-neutral.
- Screening/maneuvering cavalry is assessed as a lower threat; a committed charge receives the higher Square threat score.
- Existing automatic Square state refreshes while the mounted threat remains nearby instead of expiring after the old timeout while cavalry is still present.
- Destroyed cavalry no longer continues autonomous cavalry AI execution.
- F30M temporary Major A/B cavalry attachment and return-to-reserve behaviour remains in force.
