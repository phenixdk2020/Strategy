v00.00.09f22 - Major objective-centred formation planner
- Major ANGRIB HER now treats the clicked terrain point as the actual commander objective.
- The attack front is built behind that objective rather than being shifted to the centre of an arbitrary nearest known enemy.
- Front company slots remain side by side at approximately 60 m centre spacing.
- Unique slots are assigned by minimum total march distance across all 2-4 company permutations, reducing crossed routes.
- Reserve remains outside the front layout; a non-flanking reserve is positioned behind the recalculated front.
- Existing 09f19 flank judgement is retained.
- Each company receives an individual final facing toward the nearest known relevant enemy, so outer companies can turn inward toward a central enemy instead of all remaining parallel.
- Saved Major assignments are updated together with live missions so AI OFF/ON reattachment does not restore stale pre-09f22 positions.
- Console telemetry: HQ-FORMATION-09F22.

v00.00.09f22 - Company selection reach
- Added a 5000 m late selection layer matching the expanded battlefield/HQ raycast scale.
- Clicking a Danish company can now take selection authority from Major even when the legacy PlayerCommander 1000 m click ray would miss from a higher camera position.
- Shift/Ctrl additive selection semantics are retained.
- Console telemetry: SELECTION-09F22.

v00.00.09f22 - Building endpoint legality
- If a Line/Column destination lies inside the formation-clearance area around Farmhouse/Barn, the raw endpoint is projected to the nearest legal edge before static obstacle routing.
- This targets the observed up/down oscillation when the requested final formation physically cannot fit at the raw destination.
- The current implementation still moves one logical company around a hard blocker. Full visual sub-formation splitting around both sides of a building is deferred to a later path-threading layer.
- Console telemetry: OBSTACLE-ENDPOINT-09F22.

v00.00.09f22 - Fire range alignment
- Direct OrderAttack no longer marches to 92% of the selected fire-policy range before stopping.
- Attack stop range now equals the active CLOSE/MEDIUM/LONG fire trigger range, so the visible band and first legal volley threshold line up.

v00.00.09f22 - Unified bottom HUD
- Major and company command HUD now use the same low-profile, full-width bottom-panel concept.
- Panels sit flush to the bottom edge and are approximately 90 px high, around 20% lower than the previous HQ panel.
- Major HUD retains AI ON/OFF, DEF/BAL/OFF, six base Major orders and four-company status.
- Company HUD retains AI ON/OFF, DEF/BAL/OFF, facing, combat tuning access, fire policy and aggression.

Retained from v00.00.09f21

- 2+ selected manual AI OFF companies may still use side-by-side attack lanes against the same target.

Retained from v00.00.09f20
