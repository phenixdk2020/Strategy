v00.00.09f30d - OOB scroll + drag/drop + cavalry/HQ visual polish
- Backup before F30D: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f30c-pre-09f30d-oob-scroll-dnd-horses at F30C HEAD 1beb7d141c620fb62c703095782e56037a710547.
- Replaces the F30C OOB presentation with a wider fixed-column layout for unit name, men, status, AI and attachment state.
- Removes command-role text from the structural OOB labels. The regiment row is now simply `1. REGIMENT`; `Oberstløjtnant` is metadata/tooltip/HUD information rather than an extra line in the tree. Division/Brigade follow the same rule.
- Adds a real scroll view. The OOB header remains fixed; the hierarchy scrolls with mouse wheel/scrollbar when future units make the tree taller than the available panel height.
- Adds direct cavalry drag-and-drop attachment in the OOB. Drag Gardehusar/Dragon and drop on 1. Division, 1. Brigade, 1. Regiment, 1. Battalion or 2. Battalion to change CurrentCommandParent live.
- Attachment remains explicit with ATT in its own column. The row physically moves to the selected command parent after drop.
- Major rows expose AI/MAN in a dedicated AI column. Company/cavalry status is no longer squeezed together with men/attachment text.
- Cavalry visual density is increased from approximately one visible mounted figure per ten men to approximately one per five men: Gardehusar 120 -> 24 visible riders, Dragon 140 -> 28 visible riders.
- The additional mounted figures are inserted into the existing F30 formation lists, so Line/Column movement and Dragon dismount/remount continue to use the normal cavalry visual formation pipeline.
- Adds horse legs/hooves, neck/tail/ear detail to cavalry horses and legs/hooves/tail/ears to Brigade/Division HQ horses. HQ layout remains commander forward with staff behind; the horses are mounted staff, not separate riderless horses.
