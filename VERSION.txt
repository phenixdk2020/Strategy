v00.00.09f19 - Reserve is tactical judgement, including 4 versus 1
- A single known enemy no longer automatically disables the Major's reserve option.
- With four active companies against one known enemy company, the Major may choose 3 FRONT + 1 RESERVE when three companies are judged sufficient for the primary mission.
- Current prototype reserve gate requires healthy battalion condition and clear numerical superiority: average strength >= 68%, average cohesion >= 54 and total visible strength superiority >= x2.65.
- The freshest/cohesive company is preferred as reserve.
- The remaining three companies are re-centred side by side at the existing 60 m company spacing; there is no empty fourth-company hole in the front.
- Reserve may remain approximately 78 m behind the front or be assigned to a flank.

v00.00.09f19 - Flank is a separate decision
- Creating a reserve does NOT automatically mean flanking.
- A flank requires stronger conditions than simply holding a reserve: average strength >= 76%, average cohesion >= 62%, reserve strength >= 75%, useful enemy distance and a reasonably short route to a legal flank position.
- ATTACK permits a wider flank opportunity than DEFEND; defensive flanking represents a local counter-flank/counterattack.
- With one enemy, left/right flank selection uses the shorter legal route for the reserve rather than requiring unequal enemy counts on the two sides.
- SafeCompany endpoint logic from 09f18 is reused, so flank/reserve endpoints still avoid Farmhouse/Barn, open river and battlefield bounds.
- Console telemetry: HQ-RESERVE-09F19 shows Scenario=4v1, reserve company, strength superiority, average condition and whether Flank=True/False.

v00.00.09f18 retained - One AI concept for Major and companies
- Major and company UI use the same AI vocabulary: AI ON / AI OFF plus DEF / BAL / OFF doctrine.
- Direct manual company orders switch that company's OfficerAIController OFF BEFORE PlayerCommander consumes the order.
- While AI is OFF, the company is detached from the current Major mission and will not be re-tasked by that existing mission.
- Manually switching the company AI ON reattaches it to the last Major assignment when one exists.
- Any NEW Major order explicitly reclaims all four companies, clears manual detachment and switches company AI ON.
- Direct manual movement of the Major switches Major AI OFF, matching the same authority rule.

v00.00.09f18 retained - Major command authority
- PrototypeMajorBattalion09F18 remains the authoritative battalion behavior layer; 09f19 adds reserve-decision refinement on top.
- A new Major order clears each subordinate company's old PlayerCommander route and old destination ghost before assigning the new mission.
- Old manual destinations cannot resume after the Major mission completes.
- Major missions are reasserted while moving, so companies do not drift back to stale orders.
- ATTACK/FORSVAR companies stop manoeuvring and form Line as soon as they reach their slot OR an enemy is inside their selected fire-policy range, reducing movement under fire.
- Major attack movement uses company slots rather than shared forcedTarget pursuit, so subordinate companies do not converge on the enemy centre.

v00.00.09f18 retained - Selection, HQ movement and compact UI
- Major can be selected by normal click and by the same left-mouse drag rectangle concept used for companies.
- Selecting Major clears company selection and replaces the company bottom bar with the compact Major panel.
- Major hover shows AI state, doctrine, 3 horses, current battalion order and reserve decision.
- Right-click ground while Major is selected moves the physical Major/HQ; direct HQ movement turns Major AI OFF.
- The Major panel retains the six basic orders: ANGRIB HER, FORSVAR HER, TILBAGETRÆK HERTIL, RYK FREM HERTIL, HOLD POSITION, SAML HER.

v00.00.09f18 retained - New Major destinations and obstacle-safe endpoints
- Major orders create their own visible company path lines and formation destination footprints.
- Selecting the Major shows command-links to all subordinate companies plus their current Major-assigned destinations.
- Major assignment endpoints are checked against Farmhouse/Barn formation footprints and open river positions before use.
- The existing bridge/building route layers still own the legal route TO the selected safe endpoint.
