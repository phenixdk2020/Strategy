v00.00.09f30e - 1:1 Cavalry + Historical Visual Fidelity
- Backup before F30E: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f30d-pre-09f30e-1to1-cavalry-visuals at F30D HEAD d8d52bfbdbfac184ecabc201b021b72897c1db35.
- Tactical cavalry visual scale is now 1:1, matching the infantry direction: one simulated cavalryman = one visible mounted figure at tactical detail.
- Gardehusar Eskadron: InitialStrength 120 -> 120 visible mounted riders.
- Dragon Eskadron: InitialStrength 140 -> 140 visible mounted riders.
- Dragon dismounted representation is also filled to 1:1: 140 men on foot when SID AF is used; the 140 mounted horse figures remain physically at the horse-holder position with riders hidden.
- F30E fills the existing F30/F30D formation lists rather than creating a decorative parallel formation, so all 120/140 figures use the existing Line/Column movement pipeline.
- Existing early F30/F30D mounted figures are visually upgraded and recoloured so the full formation reads consistently instead of having a low-detail first block and a different-detail tail.
- Gardehusar visual identity: light-blue hussar silhouette, silver braid, black fur headgear, red pelisse/plume accents and sabre.
- Dragon visual identity: dark-blue coat, red facings, crested dark helmet, carbine and sabre.
- Horses use a fuller low-poly silhouette with body/chest/neck/head/muzzle, four legs and hooves, mane, tail, ears, saddle/cloth, bridle and reins. Several brown horse tones break up the previous cloned-block appearance.
- Brigade/Division mounted HQ staff receive rider legs, arms, boots, helmet/crest, collar and sabre on top of the F30D horse-leg pass; they remain mounted officer/staff groups, not horses standing beside dismounted officers.
- One-to-one formation footprint is continuously reasserted after Line/Column and mounted/dismounted transitions so the logical selection/collision footprint matches the larger visible formation.
- F30E hardening registers the visual upgrade of legacy F30/F30D riders with mountedRiders, so Dragon SID AF hides both old and new rider geometry instead of leaving ghost riders on the horses.
- WEDGE is not added as a default 1851/1864 formation. F30E retains LINE/COLUMN; ECHELON LEFT/RIGHT remains the next historically grounded cavalry-formation candidate.
- OOB scroll, drag/drop attachment, dynamic CurrentCommandParent, cavalry Officer AI and manual override remain F30D/F30C behavior and are not replaced by this visual pass.

v00.00.09f30d - OOB scroll + drag/drop + cavalry/HQ visual polish
- Backup before F30D: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f30c-pre-09f30d-oob-scroll-dnd-horses at F30C HEAD 1beb7d141c620fb62c703095782e56037a710547.
- Replaces the F30C OOB presentation with a wider fixed-column layout for unit name, men, status, AI and attachment state.
- Removes command-role text from the structural OOB labels. The regiment row is now simply `1. REGIMENT`; `Oberstløjtnant` is metadata/tooltip/HUD information rather than an extra line in the tree. Division/Brigade follow the same rule.
- Adds a real scroll view. The OOB header remains fixed; the hierarchy scrolls with mouse wheel/scrollbar when future units make the tree taller than the available panel height.
- Adds direct cavalry drag-and-drop attachment in the OOB. Drag Gardehusar/Dragon and drop on 1. Division, 1. Brigade, 1. Regiment, 1. Battalion or 2. Battalion to change CurrentCommandParent live.
- Attachment remains explicit with ATT in its own column. The row physically moves to the selected command parent after drop.
- Major rows expose AI/MAN in a dedicated AI column. Company/cavalry status is no longer squeezed together with men/attachment text.
- Cavalry visual density is increased from approximately one visible mounted figure per ten men to approximately one per five men: Gardehusar 120 -> 24 visible riders, Dragon 140 -> 28 visible riders.
- The additional mounted figures are inserted into the existing F30 formation lists, so Line/Column movement and Dragon dismount/remount continue to use the normal cavalry visual formation pipeline.
- Adds horse legs/hooves, neck/tail/ear detail to cavalry horses and legs/hooves/tail/ears to Brigade/Division HQ horses. HQ layout remains commander forward with staff behind; the horses are mounted staff, not separate riderless horses.
