v00.00.09f20 - Major/company selection handoff
- Fixed selection authority after selecting the Major.
- A successfully selected Danish company now immediately takes UI/selection authority away from the Major.
- This applies to both normal click selection and the existing left-mouse box selection because the 09f20 handoff runs after PlayerCommander, box selection and Major UI have processed the frame.
- Selecting a company restores the normal company bottom bar; selecting Major still clears company selection and opens the Major panel.
- Merely selecting a company does NOT switch its AI OFF. The AI is switched OFF only when the player gives that selected company a direct manual order, preserving the 09f18 authority rule.

v00.00.09f20 - Player-issued Major mission is commander intent
- A player-issued Major order such as FORSVAR HER or ANGRIB HER is now treated as the commander's mission intent.
- With Major AI ON, the Major may still decide subordinate placement, reserve and flank usage, but ThinkMajor is prevented from silently replacing that player mission with a different autonomous mission.
- This fixes the case where the player ordered FORSVAR HER and the Major later abandoned it because autonomous AI selected another task.
- A new explicit player Major order replaces the previous commander intent.

v00.00.09f20 - Doctrine response
- Changing Major doctrine while a player mission is active immediately re-plans the SAME mission/objective rather than discarding it.
- Example: FORSVAR HER remains FORSVAR HER when switching between OFF/BAL/DEF; doctrine changes how the Major interprets/executes the mission, not the objective itself.
- With Major AI ON and NO explicit player mission, switching to DEF immediately issues a defensive posture at the battalion's current centre and prevents the previous offensive plan from continuing.
- With no explicit player mission, switching to BAL/OFF returns autonomous mission selection to ThinkMajor immediately.
- Console telemetry: MAJOR-INTENT-09F20 and SELECTION-09F20.

v00.00.09f19 retained - Reserve is tactical judgement, including 4 versus 1
- A single known enemy does not automatically disable the Major's reserve option.
- With four active companies against one known enemy company, the Major may choose 3 FRONT + 1 RESERVE when three companies are judged sufficient for the primary mission.
- Current prototype reserve gate requires healthy battalion condition and clear numerical superiority: average strength >= 68%, average cohesion >= 54 and total visible strength superiority >= x2.65.
- The freshest/cohesive company is preferred as reserve.
- The remaining three companies are re-centred side by side at the existing 60 m company spacing.
- Reserve may remain approximately 78 m behind the front or be assigned to a flank.

v00.00.09f19 retained - Flank is a separate decision
- Creating a reserve does NOT automatically mean flanking.
- A flank requires stronger conditions than simply holding a reserve: average strength >= 76%, average cohesion >= 62%, reserve strength >= 75%, useful enemy distance and a reasonably short route to a legal flank position.
- ATTACK permits a wider flank opportunity than DEFEND; defensive flanking represents a local counter-flank/counterattack.
- SafeCompany endpoint logic from 09f18 is reused, so flank/reserve endpoints avoid Farmhouse/Barn, open river and battlefield bounds.

v00.00.09f18 retained - Unified AI authority
- Major and company UI use the same AI vocabulary: AI ON / AI OFF plus DEF / BAL / OFF doctrine.
- Direct manual company orders switch that company's OfficerAIController OFF before PlayerCommander consumes the order.
- While AI is OFF, the company is detached from the current Major mission.
- Manually switching the company AI ON reattaches it to its last Major assignment when one exists.
- Any NEW Major order reclaims all four companies, clears manual detachment and switches company AI ON.
- Direct manual movement of the Major switches Major AI OFF.
- A new Major order clears old company PlayerCommander routes/ghosts and assigns fresh Major-owned destinations.
