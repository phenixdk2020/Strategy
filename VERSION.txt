v10i PURPOSE:
- Promote provider 9 Imagery from a fixed Denmark rectangle to the first camera-driven world basemap pilot.
- Keep the other ten basemap candidates available for comparison.
- Preserve historical/gameplay data as separate WGS84 layers; modern imagery is presentation/reference only.
- Prove that the campaign camera can move from Denmark to Europe and a world overview without changing scene.

PROVIDER 9 WORLD STREAMING:
- Source remains Esri World Imagery XYZ tiles for the prototype comparison layer.
- The visible tile set is calculated from camera center, aspect ratio and orthographic zoom.
- Dynamic LOD selects Web-Mercator zoom levels z2 through z12.
- Tile budget is capped at 96 visible/requested tiles by lowering LOD when necessary.
- Up to 8 tile requests run concurrently.
- Tiles nearest the camera center are requested first.
- New tile generations are built hidden and atomically swapped in only when complete.
- The previous generation stays visible while a replacement LOD/area loads.
- Camera movement during a pending generation invalidates that generation and rebuilds after a short stability delay.
- Runtime-created mesh/material/texture objects are explicitly released when an old generation is replaced.
- Existing persistent provider cache is reused by zoom/x/y.

WORLD CAMERA:
- Starts at Denmark: 10.20 E / 56.10 N, orthographic size 43.
- Home = Denmark overview.
- PageUp = Europe overview.
- End = world overview.
- WASD / arrow keys = pan.
- Middle mouse drag = grab/pan.
- Mouse wheel = zoom.
- Longitude is currently clamped to -179.5..179.5 and latitude to -80..80 for the pilot.

LOD BANDS:
- World overview: z2-z4.
- Continental/Europe: approximately z4-z6.
- Country/Denmark: approximately z7-z8.
- Regional/local: z9-z12.
- The tile-budget guard may lower one or more levels on wide aspect ratios.

PROJECTION NOTE:
- Gameplay coordinates remain WGS84.
- Provider 9 imagery tiles are Web Mercator source tiles and are placed into the existing Denmark-first linear Unity WGS84 render projection.
- This is sufficient for the world-streaming technology pilot but is NOT the final global projection architecture.
- Dateline wrapping, polar treatment, globe curvature and floating-origin support remain future work before a production global campaign.
