v00.00.09l1 - Unity 6.6 compile compatibility fix
- Fixes CS0104 in PrototypeFullScaleOOB09K.cs by explicitly aliasing Object to UnityEngine.Object where System and UnityEngine are both imported.
- Fixes CS0619 in PrototypeDualStandards09L.cs by removing obsolete GetInstanceID() usage entirely.
- Dual-standard wave phase is now derived deterministically from regiment starting position; no EntityId/object-instance identifier is required for this presentation-only value.
- No Brigade AI, OOB strength, special-arms, 1:1 renderer, combat, navigation or movement behavior is changed by 09l1.

v00.00.09l - Historical Danish brigade pilot
- The Danish side now represents the historically documented 7. Brigade structure around 1 Feb 1864:
  * 1. Infanteri-Regiment: approx. 1,540 men.
  * 11. Infanteri-Regiment: approx. 1,584 men.
- The stable 09k technical renderer key for the second Danish slot remains "5. Regiment" internally in this prototype; OOB identity, Brigade HQ, dual standards and display metadata identify it as 11. Infanteri-Regiment.
- This technical/display split is temporary until 09m introduces stable Unit IDs independent of display name.
- The Prussian pair (8th + 18th) remains explicitly QA rather than being presented as a sourced historical brigade.
- Total infantry in the pilot: 8,024.

v00.00.09l - Historical identity data
- PrototypeRegimentOOB09K now carries:
  * OfficialName1864
  * TraditionalName
  * HistoricalLineage
  * RegimentalRomanNumeral
  * AuthorizedStrength / PresentStrength
- 1. Infanteri-Regiment carries the Danske Livregiment tradition.
- 11. Infanteri-Regiment carries a Falstersk/Jysk lineage label based on the current historical working paper.
- Reusable identity metadata remains defined for 5. Infanteri-Regiment / Sjællandske Livregiment for later OOB Designer scenarios.
- Battalion/company structure remains 2x4 for Denmark and 3x4 for Prussia; company parent is explicit data and must not later be inferred from company number alone.

v00.00.09l - Brigade HQ
- One physical Brigade HQ is created per side.
- Danish HQ: 7. Brigade.
- Prussian HQ: Prussian Brigade (QA).
- Brigade HQ is represented by six mounted staff figures:
  1. brigade commander,
  2. adjutant,
  3. staff officer,
  4. staff officer,
  5. courier,
  6. courier.
- Regimental three-rider HQ groups from 09k remain in place.
- Brigade HQ is a separate command entity and does not replace the subordinate regiment HQs.

v00.00.09l - Brigade objective / reserve AI
- Brigade missions in the first pilot: Hold, DefendArea, AttackCaptureArea.
- Regiment roles: Reserve, Support, Engaged, Manoeuvre, Withdraw.
- Danish Brigade AI starts OFF so player manual control remains the default.
- F11 toggles Danish Brigade AI ON/OFF.
- Prussian QA brigade starts Brigade AI ON.
- The brigade initially commits one regiment and holds the second as reserve.
- Reserve is committed automatically when the primary regiment routes, falls below approximately 72% strength, morale/cohesion deteriorates, or an attack shows no meaningful progress for about 12 seconds.
- A committed reserve becomes SUPPORT and later ENGAGED near the objective.
- Brigade command issues missions through OfficerAIController. It does not write Regiment.destination directly.

v00.00.09l - Dual standards / flags
- Every infantry regiment receives two visible standards:
  1. national/army identity flag,
  2. regimental identity flag.
- Danish national flag is Dannebrog.
- Danish regimental flag remains Dannebrog-based in the 1864 direction and shows the regiment Roman numeral.
- Regiment/tradition identity is shown separately, for example Danske Livregiment, and the data model supports Sjællandske Livregiment for 5. Infanteri-Regiment in later scenarios.
- Older single-standard prototype renderers are hidden rather than deleted.
- The dual flags receive a subtle independent wave motion.
- Historical caution remains: later traditions/mottoes must not be represented as official 1864 facts without source verification.

v00.00.09l - Cavalry horse state
- Cavalry manpower and serviceable horses are now separate values.
- Danish cavalry pilot: approximately 135 personnel, 120 serviceable horses, 120 mounted effective; unmounted personnel are shown as a rear foot group.
- Prussian dragoon squadron remains QA: 160 personnel, 155 serviceable horses/mounted effective.
- MountedEffective is capped by both Personnel and HorsesAvailable.
- Dismount/remount, horse holders, horse casualties, cavalry fatigue and combat remain later gates.

v00.00.09l - Danish field artillery correction
- Danish field battery is updated from the old 6-gun/120-man QA placeholder to the current historical working baseline:
  * 8 guns,
  * approximately 190 personnel.
- Prussian battery remains QA at 6 guns / 120 personnel until the matching historical source is locked.
- Artillery still has no full fire/limber/ammunition model in 09l.
