v00.01.00d1 - Ghost Destination + Facing + March Test
- Builds directly on v00.01.00c5.
- This is the first Gate D movement slice.
- Active OOB remains exactly eight independent Companies: four Danish and four Prussian.
- Actual tactical manpower remains Denmark 759 + Prussia 812 = 1571 men.
- Stable UnitID/OOB and clean Company-first ownership remain unchanged.
- Company is the sole owner of its tactical world pose and movement state.
- RMB now creates a destination ghost instead of being ignored.
- RMB-down places the destination centre.
- Hold RMB and drag to rotate the ghost to the desired final facing.
- F while the ghost is active toggles its target formation Line <-> Column.
- Release RMB confirms the order.
- The ghost rectangle shows the final footprint and a facing arrow shows the final direction before confirmation.
- Multiple selected Companies receive corresponding ghost footprints while preserving their relative group offsets.
- After confirmation, Companies move to the destination instead of teleporting.
- Position, facing and Line/Column reforming are interpolated during the order.
- Movement duration is derived from distance, turn angle and formation-change time.
- QA march speed is 1.35 m/s and turn speed is 55 degrees/s.
- Selection footprint, collider and outline interpolate toward the target formation footprint.
- Old Z/X snap/animated-turn drill controls are disabled in normal D1 play; facing is now specified by the ghost order.
- Old F instant/standalone drill is disabled in normal D1 play; F changes the active ghost formation.
- Visual ratio settings remain available at 1:1, 1:2, 1:3, 1:4, 1:5, 1:10 and 1:20.
- Visual ratio remains presentation-only; OOB/manpower, later ammo, casualties, morale and combat state use real strength.
- Representative soldiers remain sampled across the full real-strength formation.
- New D1 renderer adds articulated upper/lower limbs, neck, nose/face silhouette, shoulders, distinct headgear, equipment and clearer weapon silhouette.
- Marching/reforming/turning soldiers receive a procedural gait with leg swing, arm swing and body bob instead of sliding as static figures.
- Prussian close silhouette includes a distinct spike helmet profile; Danish headgear remains visually separate.
- Permanent world labels remain removed.
- Hover info remains available through H.
- F10 visual-ratio settings remain available.
- c5 QA Lab remains available, but Auto Drill should not be run while testing D1 movement orders.
- No flags/standards in this revision.
- No obstacle avoidance, bridge/river routing or V3 detour logic yet; D1 validates the direct order/ghost/movement contract first.
- Combat, Officer AI, Brigade AI, artillery and cavalry remain inactive.

Controls
- LMB: select Danish Company.
- Shift+LMB: add to selection.
- Ctrl+LMB: toggle selection.
- LMB drag: box selection.
- Esc: clear selection / cancel active ghost.
- RMB down: place destination ghost.
- Hold RMB + drag: set final facing.
- F while ghost active: target Line <-> Column.
- RMB release: confirm order and march/reform to the ghost.
- F10: open/close visual ratio settings.
- H: hover info ON/OFF.
- V: Debug/Clean view.
- F8: c5 QA Lab panel.
- F6: visual-ratio benchmark.
