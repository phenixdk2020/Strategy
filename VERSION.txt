Design manual baseline: v00.02.08
Unity editor baseline: 6000.6.0f1 (Unity 6.6)

Repository layout: Unity project root = repository root
Built-in modules enabled: IMGUI, Particle System, Physics, Audio
Previous gate: v00.00.08 reload/experience, 0-hit volley, 'Ramte N' and simple casualty visual retained
Scenario: Danish test regiments are defenders; Prussian test regiments are attackers
Battlefield: doubled from 180x120 to 360x240 with expanded camera bounds and greater starting separation
Fire geometry: directional 120-degree forward fire fan follows regiment facing; no 360-degree firing ring
Range bands: Close <= 50% EffectiveRange; Medium <= EffectiveRange; Long <= MaximumRange
Range fan visibility: Close/Medium arcs and outer Long fan are terrain-following, unlit, thicker and higher-contrast so no side disappears into uneven ground
Fire policy: HOLD / CLOSE / MEDIUM / LONG determines when a regiment may open fire
Accuracy: continuous distance curve; closer targets are progressively easier to hit with no hard accuracy jump at range-band boundaries
Fire eligibility MVP: target must be inside selected fire-policy range and inside the forward fire arc
Reload: weapon profile base reload + bounded regiment Experience modifier retained from v00.00.08
Volley feedback revision: TEST layer detects every resolved volley and shows temporary Ramte N including Ramte 0, so feedback does not disappear merely because later volleys score zero hits
Ammunition TEST model: each regiment starts at 60 carried rounds per man; one volley consumes one round per man; display is rounds per man, not total cartridges; no automatic regeneration
Officer assignment: every prototype regiment receives an OfficerProfile + OfficerAIController at runtime
Officer core stats: Leadership, Inspiration, Tactical Skill, Initiative, Staff/Command Skill, Discipline/Obedience, Aggressiveness/Caution, Composure/Nerve
Officer background: Experience is separate and primarily reduces decision noise
Player delegation: I toggles AI UNIT ON/OFF for selected Danish regiment(s); A remains camera-left in WASD
Officer AI doctrine menu: DEFENSIVE / BALANCED / OFFENSIVE applies to selected Danish regiment(s)
Commander aggression intent: 0-100 slider biases cautious/aggressive execution, while officer Aggressiveness remains dominant (70% officer / 30% order before doctrine bias)
Fire discipline menu: HOLD / CLOSE / MEDIUM / LONG applies to selected Danish regiment(s)
Battle HUD revision: full regiment info is shown only for selected or mouse-hovered units; compact box includes current/initial strength, Tab/losses, Ammo rounds per man, weapon/reload/formation, fire ranges, morale/cohesion and compact Officer AI state
Command UI revision: selected-unit Officer AI controls are a fixed responsive bottom command bar only 60-66 px high with two control rows and no third status row
Time HUD revision: battle date/time and PAUSE/x0.5/x1/x2/x5/x20 are in one persistent centred top bar; Space and 0/1/2/3/4 remain shortcuts
UI input isolation: clicks inside time controls or the bottom command bar do not issue battlefield selection/move/attack orders
Multi-selection: Ctrl+left-click or Shift+left-click adds/removes Danish regiments from the current selection
Formation control: manual F/C selects line/column; Officer AI may use column for longer approach marches and returns to line near engagement based on Tactical Skill and distance
Formation-line order: hold right mouse button on terrain and drag a line; live terrain-following preview rotates with the drag, and release assigns selected regiments evenly along that line
Formation-line facing: reversing the right-drag direction flips the final facing; minimum 22-unit centre spacing prevents selected regiments stacking on top of each other
Order ghosts: every move order receives a terrain-following ghost path from regiment to destination plus a persistent destination footprint showing where and how the regiment is expected to stand
Facing order: Z rotates selected regiment(s) 15 degrees left; X rotates 15 degrees right; matching DREJ -15 / DREJ +15 buttons are in the compact bottom command bar; if a move ghost exists the final ghost facing is rotated instead of losing the movement order
Casualty visuals: representative bodies accumulate as losses occur, approximately one visible body per ten losses; bodies are spread across/behind the frontage and remain where the casualties occurred even while formations stand and exchange fire
Enemy AI: Prussian officers start Offensive with order aggression 65 and Medium fire policy; 18th uses a flank/approach waypoint before reassessing attack
Enemy behaviour rule: attackers close to officer/order preferred range, deploy to line near engagement, may stabilise under morale pressure, and do not receive combat-stat cheats
Difficulty: F6 Easy, F7 Normal, F8 Hard; affects enemy AI reaction/decision noise only, not combat stats or hidden knowledge
AI telemetry: AI-DIAG exposes unit, officer, doctrine, order aggression, effective aggression, fire policy, mission, task and reason
Pause semantics: AI, movement, reload, combat and battle clock stop together; camera/UI remain usable
Slow tactical mode: x0.5 gives twice the real-world reaction time per unit of simulation time without changing combat stats
