v00.00.09f14 - Legacy 16-hit ceiling calibration layer
- Regiment.FireVolley still contains a legacy hard ceiling of 16 resolved hits.
- Added PrototypeLethalityCalibration09F14 at execution order 37100.
- The layer only supplements a volley if the original kernel actually reaches the 16-hit ceiling.
- It never reduces or rewrites uncapped lower-hit volleys.
- Supplemental hits are physically limited by firing men and target strength.
- Original volley shock is not applied twice; supplemental hits use zero extra shock.
- This is explicitly temporary until the authoritative hit resolver is cleaned up and the legacy 16-hit ceiling is removed from the kernel.

v00.00.09f14 - Definitive calibration telemetry
- Existing VOLLEY-QA-09F13 remains for raw/kernel telemetry and audio.
- New definitive line is VOLLEY-CAL-09F14.
- It logs shooter, target, actual distance, band, firing men, BaseHit, range factor, quality, Expected, KernelHits, ExtraHits, TotalHits and TargetRemaining.
- For B-271 tuning, use TotalHits from VOLLEY-CAL-09F14.
- Run at least 10-20 comparable salves per range band before the next tuning decision.

v00.00.09f14 - Later weapon-model direction
- Final ranged combat must not use one universal accuracy curve.
- Weapon profiles shall later distinguish at least older smoothbore muskets from rifled/tapriffel/Minié-like weapons and other relevant systems.
- Each weapon profile can then own its own distance/accuracy behavior while common formation, quality, smoke, cover and movement factors remain shared.
