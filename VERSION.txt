v00.00.09f7 - Compact musket-range test
- Battlefield scale remains 1 Unity unit = 1 metre.
- Current TEST fire distances are Close 40 m, Medium/Effective 80 m and Long/Maximum 115 m.
- Long/Maximum is intentionally reduced from the 09f6 400 m visual test to match the desired tactical battlefield scale.
- The visible fire sector is reduced from 120 degrees total to 70 degrees total (+/-35 degrees).
- PrototypeFireArcGuard09F7 prevents the current 1v1 prototype from firing when its nearest in-range opponent is outside the new visible +/-35 degree sector.
- These remain prototype gameplay/range values; later historical weapon profiles are data/research work.

v00.00.09f7 - Volley, reload and black-powder smoke
- Visible soldier states now include AWAIT, AIM, FIRE and RELOAD.
- Aim raises the rifle toward the target-facing direction.
- Fire uses a short recoil pose.
- Reload returns the weapon to an upright loading position before the next shot.
- A volley creates black-powder smoke across the full company frontage rather than from only a few isolated points.
- Smoke remains in world space after emission, so a company moving later does not drag the old cloud with it.

v00.00.09f7 - Walking, forced march and rout running
- Normal movement now has a visible walking gait rather than soldiers sliding over terrain.
- Forced March is available with the G hotkey and the bottom-bar TVANG G button.
- Danish current TEST speeds: normal walk 3.20 m/s, forced march 4.25 m/s, routed run 5.20 m/s.
- Prussian current TEST speeds: normal walk 3.35 m/s, forced march 4.45 m/s, routed run 5.40 m/s.
- Forced March adds 0.22 cohesion points/second on top of existing movement cohesion cost while the unit is moving.
- Routed/panicked units automatically abandon Forced March and use the faster RUN state while fleeing.
- Visible gait amplitude, body lean and weapon carry differ between Walk, Forced March and Run.

v00.00.09f7 - Casualty placement
- New fallen soldiers are created from actual formation slots at the moment casualties are received.
- Casualty placement has only about +/-0.30 m local jitter around the formation slot.
- Fallen soldiers therefore appear in or immediately beside the line/column rather than several metres away.
- Fallen visuals remain in world space when the surviving company moves on.

v00.00.09f7 - Exact formation bounds
- Selection and destination footprints are calculated from min/max of all current formation slots.
- This fixes the 09f6 Column error where the rectangle was centred on the regiment pivot even though the marching column extends behind that pivot.
- Line and Column selection rectangles now use the actual formation centre, width and depth.
- Destination ghosts apply the same centre offset and therefore match the requested final formation.
- The yellow selection orb follows the actual formation centre.

v00.00.09f6 retained

- Player and AI movement share the same march-column policy.
- River routing remains bridge-only and scenery remains pass-through.
- Selection boxes remain terrain-following on all four edges.
