V10N6H CITY ART VISIBILITY:
- CampaignCityArtV010N6H supersedes the n6g city-art renderer at runtime.
- The renderer uses a dedicated PROJECT1864/CampaignCityBillboard shader.
- Shader contract: Cull Off, ZWrite Off, ZTest Always, alpha blending.
- City art therefore cannot disappear because of terrain depth or backface culling.
- A bottom-anchored billboard mesh is used rather than Unity's default centered Quad primitive.
- Proposal-3 Resources textures remain preferred.
- If a texture is unavailable, a visible generated fallback settlement is used.
- A/B/C base scales: A=4.80, B=3.65, C=2.65.
- Old CITY_ICON_ and CITY_ART_ children are removed before n6h installation.
- Old round city renderer remains hidden; no green TownGround is generated.
- Stable BoxCollider click targets remain on the city marker parents.

V10N6H AMT BOUNDARY CONTINUITY:
- n6g source parish polygons remain the authoritative geometry and click source.
- CampaignAmtBoundaryContinuityV010N6H does not alter Amt ownership.
- It only bridges short line-chain endpoint gaps when both boundary directions are compatible.
- Maximum bridge distance: 0.040 geographic degrees.
- Direction threshold: dot >= 0.72 at both endpoints.
- This targets visual gaps caused by neighbouring parish polygons using different vertex subdivision.
- Source parish geometry, click resolver and ZoneId ownership remain unchanged.
