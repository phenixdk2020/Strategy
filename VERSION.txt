v00.00.09f29v - Tactical map, semantic zoom, OOB status and Square corrections
- Backup created before changes: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f29u-pre-09f29v-minimap-semantic-zoom.
- Tactical map now uses fixed whole-battlefield bounds instead of recalculating around active units.
- Camera position/visible footprint moves across the fixed map as a white viewport marker.
- Major HQs are marked II and Regimental HQ is marked III on the tactical map.
- Tactical-map interaction remains: single-click own company/HQ selects only; double-click selects and moves behind; empty/enemy click moves camera to map area.
- Legacy F29P/F29T tactical-map drawing/interaction layers are disabled by the F29V map owner to avoid duplicate maps and mismatched bounds.
- Semantic zoom is unified by F29V: company NATO counters are continuous from 175 m upward.
- 3D units and NATO counters coexist from 175 m to 314 m.
- At 315 m and above 3D unit/HQ meshes are suppressed while NATO counters remain visible.
- HQ NATO counters remain visible from 72 m upward.
- Legacy F29D/F29Q semantic layers are disabled by the F29V semantic owner to remove the 235 m handoff gap/duplication risk.
- OOB company rows now expose tactical status rather than generic HOLD only: KLAR, RYKKER, FORSVAR, RESERVE, FLANKE, SQUARE, KAMP, UNDER ILD, CHARGE, MELEE or ROUT.
- FORSVAR means assigned/arrived defensive mission; it does not claim an entrenchment bonus. A future trench/entrenchment mechanic may add GRAVET NED as a distinct state.
- Square correction keeps the actual SquareOutline visible.
- The generic Major/HQ mission Footprint rectangle is hidden while the company is in Square.
- Legacy Square Close/Medium/Long 360-degree rings and normal forward range fans remain suppressed in Square.
- Four 90-degree Square fire sectors remain authoritative (front/right/rear/left).
- Square fire requires an actual active enemy on the relevant face and inside the unit's current fire-policy range; Regiment.CanFireAt is used as the final volley gate.
- Independent per-face reload timers remain in use while the legacy global Regiment fire timer is parked during Square.
- F29S Square sector owner is disabled by F29V to avoid two competing Square fire owners.
- No movement authority, bridge-only river rule, charge/melee ownership, Side Step ownership or command hierarchy is intentionally changed.
