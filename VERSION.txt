v10j DEM RENDER + PRESENTATION FIX:
- First Campaign4 Play Mode screenshot confirmed that the perspective camera was active, but the DEM terrain itself was effectively invisible and the screen was dominated by the blue sea board plus overlapping labels.
- Root cause: inherited v10g DEM tile triangle winding produced downward-facing normals in the X/Z projection; back-face culling therefore hid the land mesh from the above-ground perspective camera.
- Campaign4DemMeshRepairV010J.cs watches streamed DEM tile meshes and reverses only meshes whose calculated average normal points downward, then recalculates normals/bounds.
- Campaign4PresentationV010J.cs hides the inherited v10g TRUE 11 BASEMAP LAB panel after provider 1 is activated.
- Perspective semantic city labels now show top 5 at strategic range, top 15 at operational range and all 40 only near city scale.
- Legacy zone-label overlap is suppressed at strategic and very close zoom through the compatibility zoom-band proxy.
- A compact Campaign4 status/control line replaces the large provider-lab overlay.

v10k UNITY 6.6 COMPATIBILITY FIX:
- Unity 6.6 reports Object.GetInstanceID() as obsolete/error-level and requires newer entity APIs.
- Campaign4DemMeshRepairV010J.cs no longer calls GetInstanceID().
- Processed DEM meshes are tracked directly in HashSet<Mesh>, which is sufficient because each streamed DEM tile owns a mesh instance for its lifetime.
- DEM winding repair behaviour is otherwise unchanged.
