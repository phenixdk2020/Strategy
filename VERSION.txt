v00.00.09f8 - Shorter fire-distance test
- Battlefield scale remains 1 Unity unit = 1 metre.
- Current TEST fire distances are now Close 35 m, Medium/Effective 70 m and Long/Maximum 100 m.
- Long/Maximum is intentionally between the former 80 m Medium and 115 m Long visual lines from 09f7.
- Short and Medium are shortened proportionally for a tighter tactical battlefield.
- The fire sector remains 70 degrees total (+/-35 degrees).
- PrototypeRangeTuning09F8 runs after the 09f7 range tuner and is the active 09f8 range authority.

v00.00.09f8 - Shared cone side boundaries
- Close, Medium and Long range fans now use the same left and right boundary directions.
- Each fan originates across the full firing frontage instead of pinching inward from a single centre point.
- The yellow and orange fan sides therefore align with the red Long fan side rays.
- PrototypeFireVisuals09F8 runs after the 09f7 tactical visual layer and owns final range-fan geometry/visibility.

v00.00.09f8 - Cone/fire hidden until formation is ready
- Fire-range cones are hidden while the company is in Column.
- When Column changes back to Line, cones remain hidden while the physical soldier slots reform.
- Reform wait time is estimated from the furthest soldier-slot movement at the same 2.8 m/s visual reform speed used by the company renderer.
- Firing is also temporarily blocked while in Column or during the Line reform window, keeping gameplay consistent with the visible cone state.
- Once Line is physically ready, the cone and normal fire policy become available again.

v00.00.09f8 - Morale temporarily disabled for hit QA
- Morale is locked to 100 during this TEST revision so accumulated morale loss does not obscure raw casualty testing.
- Morale therefore does not accumulate between volleys during the 09f8 QA pass.
- Cohesion remains active and still influences fire quality.
- Distance accuracy remains active and continues to decline continuously with distance.
- SHORT/MEDIUM/LONG are therefore still meaningfully different even though morale is temporarily bypassed.
- New HITTEST-09F8 telemetry logs target, attacker, distance, range band, hits/losses, remaining strength and cohesion whenever casualties occur.
