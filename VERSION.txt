v00.00.09f30c - Cavalry Officer AI + Dynamic Attachment + Manual Override
- Backup before F30C: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f30b-pre-09f30c-cavalry-ai-attachments at F30B HEAD 407fa095598753e06d155b1e84a6aa89a09821d2.
- Adds PrototypeCavalryOfficerAI09F30C. Gardehusar and Dragon cavalry officers start AI ON for QA and independently seek a FLANK or REAR approach before committing a mounted charge.
- Cavalry AI evaluates live Prussian infantry targets, strongly avoids charging a ready Square, prefers enemies already locally engaged, moves in Column during approach, reforms Line before charge, and uses the existing F30 FRONT/FLANK/REAR charge resolver at contact.
- Cavalry AI replans if the target moves materially. River/open-water safety remains owned by the existing F30 bridge-only mounted routing; maneuver endpoints are kept outside the visual river corridor and inside battlefield bounds.
- Direct player command authority is explicit. A right-click battlefield order or cavalry bottom-HUD command disables that unit's cavalry AI, and the player can re-enable AI from the cavalry HUD at any time.
- Adds live CurrentCommandParent cycling for cavalry: 1. Division -> 1. Brigade -> 1. Regiment -> Major A / 1. Battalion -> Major B / 2. Battalion -> back to Division. OrganicParent remains 1. Division / Cavalry.
- If cavalry is attached directly to Major A/B, its local cavalry AI only acts while that Major's Battalion AI is ON. If the parent Major AI is OFF, cavalry reports WAIT PARENT AI instead of silently ignoring command authority.
- Replaces the fixed F30B higher OOB presentation with F30C dynamic OOB. The cavalry row physically moves under Division, Brigade, Regiment, Major A or Major B as CurrentCommandParent changes and is marked [ATT].
- Cavalry OOB row also exposes AI/MAN state. Single-click selection and double-click camera behavior remain unchanged.
- Symmetric command-tree rendering remains active for any Danish selection. F30C support links now originate from the actual CurrentCommandParent, including Major A/B.
- The selected cavalry bottom HUD adds AI ON/OFF, current attachment parent and live AI phase/target status.
- Manual control remains available at every level: higher HQ missions, Regiment/Major commands, direct company control, and direct cavalry move/formation/charge all continue to use player authority when explicitly issued.
- F30C does NOT yet add mounted firearms, dismounted Dragon fire, horse casualties, persistent cavalry melee, or artillery. Those remain subsequent combined-arms work.
