v00.00.09f29t - Interactive tactical map + command selection
- Adds click interaction to the existing F29P tactical map without replacing its renderer.
- Single-click on a Danish company selects the company only; the camera remains at the current battle location.
- Double-click on the same Danish company selects it and moves the camera behind its current facing.
- The same single-click/double-click rule applies to Major A, Major B and the Oberstløjtnant/regimental HQ.
- Clicking empty map space moves the camera focus to the corresponding battlefield position.
- Clicking a red enemy marker does not select the enemy; it moves the camera to that area.
- Map interaction uses the same dynamic bounds as the visible tactical map.
- Map clicks are isolated from PlayerCommander and box-selection input for that frame.
- A pending legacy F9 FORSVAR HER / ANGRIB / EROBR HER order is preserved and may not be accidentally resolved by clicking the minimap.
- Existing FOCUS/BAG buttons, M toggle, F/Shift+F, F1/F2/F3 and N camera controls remain unchanged.
- OOB selection remains: single-click select-only, double-click navigate behind entity.
- F29R yellow map rectangles remain crop-field terrain/concealment overlays, not selection areas.
- F29S Square sector fire remains authoritative and unchanged.
- Backup: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f29s-pre-09f29t-tactical-map-interaction.

v00.00.09f29s - Square sector fire + OOB/test-UI polish
- Adds PrototypeSquareSectorFire09F29S as the authoritative Square fire/presentation layer.
- Square uses four fixed 90-degree faces: FRONT, RIGHT, REAR and LEFT.
- Each face performs its own target selection and keeps its own reload clock.
- Existing Square accuracy/firing-men scaling yields approximately 24.4 percent of company strength per face, treated as approximately 25 percent.
- Square heading is locked while active so the complete formation does not rotate toward one threat.
- Legacy normal forward fire fans and old Square 360-degree rings/outline are suppressed during ready Square state.
- Close/Medium/Long sector visuals are drawn separately for all four faces when the selected Square has range display active.
- Regiment.FireVolley remains the authoritative volley resolver, preserving existing accuracy, terrain, morale, smoke and casualty processing.
- OOB single-click changes command selection without moving the camera; double-click deliberately navigates behind the selected company/HQ.
- Adds the compact collapsed TEST FJENDE panel and suppresses the old wide enemy-spawner test bar where found.
- Square and TEST UI changes do not become movement owners.
