Campaign startup: Denmark is the only active/playable vertical-slice nation while the common data model retains the already-decided future nations
World geography architecture: authoritative geographic identity is WGS84 longitude/latitude; Unity X/Z is only a local render projection and may be retiled/recentered later without rewriting campaign state
World expansion rule: the geography architecture is intended to expand from Denmark to Europe and theoretically the whole world by loading additional geographic tiles/data layers against the same WGS84 model
Real map source baseline: Denmark coastline/island geometry is derived from Natural Earth 1:50m Admin-0 DNK geometry; Natural Earth is the physical geographic base layer, not the historical political layer
Real map content baseline: Jutland, Zealand, Funen, Lolland, Langeland, Falster/Moen group, Amager, Aeroe, Als, Samsoe, Laesoe and Bornholm are represented as real-geography land parts from the Natural Earth dataset
Historical boundary warning: Natural Earth country geometry is modern physical/admin reference geometry and must NOT be interpreted as exact 1851 political borders; Schleswig, Holstein, Lauenburg and other 1851 political relationships require a separate source-backed historical layer
Terrain status: base land remains flat geographic mesh with coastline; real DEM/elevation has not yet been integrated
Zone model: Denmark currently has 12 operational gameplay zone centres over the real map: Vendsyssel, Nordjylland, Midtjylland, Vestjylland, Oestjylland, Sydjylland, Fyn, Nordsjaelland, Koebenhavn, Sydsjaelland, Lolland-Falster and Bornholm
Zone historical warning: current zone centres/adjacency are gameplay scaffolding, not claimed historical administrative borders; later zone polygons will be source-backed and fitted to real geography
Zone interaction: left-click selects a zone or player formation; right-click on an adjacent zone gives a march order
Sea route rule: Bornholm is deliberately disconnected from land-march routing; naval/sea transport will be a separate route capability
Strategic movement: movement uses projected geographic distance as first travel-time basis; roads, terrain, rail, weather, fatigue, supply and staff quality are later modifiers
Campaign cities: Aalborg, Viborg, Aarhus, Esbjerg, Kolding, Fredericia, Odense, Koebenhavn, Naestved and Roenne are placed by geographic coordinates as first settlement markers
Settlement historical rule: city marker locations are geographic anchors; population, fortification, industry, rail, road, port and 1851 economic/military values require historical data before simulation claims are made
Army state: one Danish QA formation exists only to prove stable IDs, selection and geographic movement; its strength/location are explicitly not final researched 1851 OOB
Campaign clock: Space pauses; 1/2/3/4 set x1/x5/x20/x100; top bar also exposes pause/speed controls
Semantic zoom foundation: camera reports Close / Operational / Strategic zoom bands and changes army-token scale; close zoom exposes city labels, operational zoom exposes zone labels, strategic mode suppresses detailed labels
Semantic zoom target: Campaign and Battle mode share the same semantic-zoom principle; future long zoom uses NATO/APP-6-like formation symbols without altering simulation IDs/state
Formation standards: campaign formation tokens keep the v00.00.10d QA standard system as close-zoom identity aid
Regimental standards shared rule: individual regimental colours belong to stable regiment identity and must also be visible in Battle mode; campaign army/corps tokens must not display dozens of standards unless the formation is unfolded/inspected
Standards historical rule: current standard graphics are QA placeholders only; final patterns, insignia, issue dates and number of colours per formation require source-backed historical data
Campaign target loop: Grand Campaign -> movement/contact -> tactical battle -> battle result -> Grand Campaign
Campaign state continuity target: regiment/formation IDs, strength, ammunition, morale/cohesion, officer profile, attacker/defender role, zone/location and campaign date/time must survive tactical handoff and return
Campaign architecture rule: new campaign code is isolated under Assets/Scripts/Campaign where practical to reduce merge conflicts with tactical development
Tactical synchronization rule: only accepted tactical fixes are later merged/cherry-picked into campaign; campaign development must not wait on minor tactical polish
