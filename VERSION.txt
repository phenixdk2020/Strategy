v00.00.09f11 - Bridge phase-state repair
- Fixes the bridge oscillation visible in the 09f10 recording where the unit repeatedly alternated APPROACH_BRIDGE and CROSS_BRIDGE.
- Bridge traversal now uses a strict forward-only phase sequence: STAGE_BRIDGE -> APPROACH_BRIDGE -> CROSS_BRIDGE -> EXIT_BRIDGE -> DIRECT.
- Once CROSS_BRIDGE begins it cannot revert to APPROACH_BRIDGE.
- Company remains in Column through the bridge corridor and normal formation logic resumes after the far staging point.
- Existing bridge-only river rule remains active.

v00.00.09f11 - Approved hard-building routing
- Added PrototypeStaticObstacleRouting09F11 without re-enabling the old unstable V1/V3/V4 obstacle navigation stack.
- Farmhouse and Barn are now explicit hard blockers.
- Their colliders remain enabled in the 1v1 isolation scenario.
- If a destination route intersects one of these buildings, the unit inserts automatic corner detour waypoints and then resumes the original final destination.
- Line uses a larger clearance reflecting its ~48 m frontage; Column uses a narrower clearance.
- River/bridge routing has priority over static-building routing.
- Trees and fences deliberately remain pass-through until their terrain behaviour is explicitly approved.

v00.00.09f11 - Battlefield expansion
- Battlefield dimensions changed from 360 x 240 m to 1440 x 960 m.
- This is 4x larger in each linear dimension and 16x the former ground area.
- Prototype scale remains 1 Unity unit = 1 metre.
- Ground mesh uses 288 x 192 cells to limit prototype cost on the much larger map.
- RTS camera bounds expanded to roughly +/-710 m x and +/-470 m z.
- Camera movement/zoom/far clip adjusted for the larger battlefield.

v00.00.09f11 - River expansion
- River visual now spans almost the full 960 m map depth instead of only the former ~240 m battlefield.
- Visible river width increased from about 2.8 m to 4.4 m, matching the existing ~2.2 m hard-blocker half-width.
- The fixed bridge remains the only legal crossing.
