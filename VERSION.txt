v00.00.09f15 - Battlefield / camera
- Battlefield changed from 1440 x 960 m to 2880 x 1920 m.
- This is 2x each linear dimension = 4x battlefield area.
- Terrain mesh keeps roughly 5 m cells and uses UInt32 indices.
- Camera minimum height reduced from 16 m to 9.5 m for substantially closer inspection of soldiers, horses and animation.
- Camera bounds now derive from PrototypeBootstrap battlefield constants rather than the old hardcoded limits.
- First environment polish pass adds warmer lighting, soft shadows, richer ground/water/road tones, fuller tree crowns and simple farmhouse detail.

v00.00.09f15 - Wider river / bridge
- Visual river width increased from 4.4 m to 5.5 m.
- River hard-blocker half-width increased to 2.75 m to match the visual river.
- Bridge remains the only legal open-water crossing.
- Bridge visual and river navigation now share PrototypeBootstrap.StreamCenterX.
- Long-route river sampling is dynamic so the larger battlefield cannot skip the narrow river between coarse samples.

v00.00.09f15 - Active company-scale test OOB
- Denmark now keeps two active company-scale units: internal IDs 1. Regiment and 5. Regiment.
- UI presents them as 1. Kompagni and 2. Kompagni.
- Prussia keeps 8th Regiment as the single opposing company-scale test unit.
- All three active units are normalized to 190 men before the existing 09f8 range tuning layer applies.

v00.00.09f15 - Major battalion HQ
- Added PrototypeMajorHQ09F15.
- Physical HQ is a Danish Major with a mounted officer, three total horses and a small HQ/Dannebrog marker.
- Major HQ has NO autonomous AI in this build.
- Selecting Major shows a yellow HQ selection ring and direct command-links to both Danish companies.
- Major is not registered as a combat Regiment and therefore does not alter victory conditions.

v00.00.09f15 - Major HQ UI
- Added PrototypeUiTheme09F15 for a darker olive/brass command-panel look.
- Major receives a dedicated HQ panel rather than the company combat panel.
- HQ panel shows HQ AI OFF, 3 horses, both subordinate companies, strength/losses and current Major mission.
- Targeted Major orders display an order prompt plus a terrain target-circle preview.

v00.00.09f15 - Six Major orders
- ANGRIB HER: distribute companies across a battalion frontage and advance toward the chosen area; local enemy contact can convert to direct attack.
- FORSVAR HER: distribute companies around the selected defensive area and hold in Line after arrival.
- TILBAGETRÆK HERTIL: organized battalion-level fallback MVP to the selected area.
- RYK FREM HERTIL: advance the battalion to the selected area without an automatic storm order.
- HOLD POSITION: cancel active Major missions and hold both companies.
- SAML HER: concentrate both companies more tightly around the selected point.
- Subordinate Danish OfficerAIControllers are kept OFF when a Major order is issued so the Major command layer remains authoritative in this first prototype.

v00.00.09f15 - Officer hierarchy documented
- Company leader = Kaptajn.
- Battalion leader = Major.
- Regiment leader = Oberstløjtnant.
- Brigade leader = Oberst.
- Division leader = Generalmajor.
- The design manual notes that historical command appointments could be filled by officers one grade below the normal level after losses or shortages.
- Courier/order delay remains later work; the approved visual model uses a small rider marker moving along the command-link, not a terrain-pathfinding horse.
