v00.00.09f2 - 1v1 isolation scenario
- Runtime scenario contains exactly one Danish and one Prussian infantry regiment.
- Denmark keeps 1. Regiment.
- Prussia keeps 8th Regiment.
- PrototypeExpandedOOBManager is disabled before it can create the additional six QA regiments.
- Start positions are simplified to approximately x=-105 and x=+105 at z=0, facing each other.
- Telemetry prefix: SCENARIO-09F2.

v00.00.09f2 - Transparent movement baseline
- All tactical obstacle/navigation steering layers are runtime-disabled for this QA build.
- PrototypeBattlefieldNavigationManager: OFF.
- PrototypeNavigationRecoveryManager: OFF.
- PrototypeBattlefieldNavigationV3: OFF.
- PrototypeBattlefieldNavigationV4: OFF.
- PrototypeNavigation09AHotfix: OFF.
- PrototypeNavigation09BTreePassThrough: OFF.
- Trees, fence posts, Farmhouse and Barn are pass-through for movement.
- River/water is also pass-through in this isolated QA baseline.
- Scenery colliders are disabled; battlefield-ground and regiment selection colliders remain active.
- Purpose: verify command/formation movement without pathfinding or obstacle-routing interference.
- Telemetry prefix: NAV-AUTH with Build=v00.00.09f2 and Authority=DirectTransparent.

v00.00.09f2 - Enemy start gate
- Prussian Officer AI is forced to HOLD / AI OFF immediately after controller installation.
- Enemy attack does not start automatically.
- F5 or the bottom-bar START FJENDE button starts the Prussian AI.
- The same control can stop the enemy again for repeatable QA.
- Telemetry prefix: ENEMY-GATE-09F2.

v00.00.09f2 - Bottom command bar
- The old raised 09f Officer AI bar is disabled after OfficerAIController installation.
- Replacement command bar is docked directly against the physical bottom edge of the Game view with no 42 px bottom gap.
- Controls retained: selected-unit AI ON/OFF, DEF/BAL/OFF doctrine, Z/X facing, KAMP F9, aggression and HOLD/CLOSE/MED/LONG fire policy.
- New global control: START/STOP FJENDE [F5].
- Pointer isolation prevents the bottom bar from issuing battlefield orders while its controls are used.
- Telemetry prefix: UI-09F2.

v00.00.09f2 - Dual flag identity
- Each active regiment now displays a national flag plus the existing regimental standard.
- Danish national flag uses a readable Dannebrog prototype.
- Prussian national flag uses a readable black/white prototype.
- Existing regimental standard/ribbon remains the second flag and regiment identifier.
- Flag art remains QA/prototype art pending historical source validation.
- Telemetry prefix: FLAG-09F2.

Retained from v00.00.09f1
- LMB drag beyond 9 pixels performs RTS box selection.
- Shift+drag adds; Ctrl+drag toggles.
- Long manual movement uses Column outside the deployment zone.
- Manual movement deploys to the final formation within 14 m of destination or when the nearest enemy enters EffectiveRange + 12 m.
- AI approach-formation policy remains available after enemy AI is started.
- Selected-unit 120-degree Close/Medium/Long range cone remains retained from 09f.
- RMB destination/facing, Alt+RMB waypoints, route ghosts, F/C formation and Z/X facing remain retained.
- Existing combat, morale, cohesion, ammunition, melee and casualty systems remain unchanged.

Known warnings
- Unity 6.6 can still report CS0618 warnings for FindObjectsByType<T>(FindObjectsSortMode) calls inside legacy navigation helper files.
- Those helper layers are runtime-disabled in v00.00.09f2 and the warnings are not the cause of movement stalls.
- Input Manager deprecation warning is non-blocking; Input System migration remains a separate controls revision.
