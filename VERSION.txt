v00.00.09f18 - One AI concept for Major and companies
- Major and company UI now use the same AI vocabulary: AI ON / AI OFF plus DEF / BAL / OFF doctrine.
- Direct manual company orders switch that company's OfficerAIController OFF BEFORE PlayerCommander consumes the order.
- While AI is OFF, the company is detached from the current Major mission and will not be re-tasked by that existing mission.
- Manually switching the company AI ON reattaches it to the last Major assignment when one exists.
- Any NEW Major order explicitly reclaims all four companies, clears manual detachment and switches company AI ON.
- Direct manual movement of the Major switches Major AI OFF, matching the same authority rule.

v00.00.09f18 - Major command authority
- PrototypeMajorBattalion09F18 supersedes the 09f17 battalion behavior layer at runtime.
- A new Major order clears each subordinate company's old PlayerCommander route and old destination ghost before assigning the new mission.
- Old manual destinations therefore cannot resume after the Major mission completes.
- Major missions are reasserted while moving, so companies do not drift back to stale orders.
- ATTACK/FORSVAR companies stop manoeuvring and form Line as soon as they reach their slot OR an enemy is inside their selected fire-policy range, reducing movement under fire.
- Major attack movement uses company slots rather than shared forcedTarget pursuit, so subordinate companies do not converge on the enemy centre.

v00.00.09f18 - Selection, HQ movement and compact UI
- Major can be selected by normal click and by the same left-mouse drag rectangle concept used for companies.
- Selecting Major clears company selection and replaces the company bottom bar with the compact Major panel.
- Major hover shows AI state, doctrine, 3 horses, current battalion order and reserve decision.
- Right-click ground while Major is selected moves the physical Major/HQ; direct HQ movement turns Major AI OFF.
- The Major panel retains the six basic orders: ANGRIB HER, FORSVAR HER, TILBAGETRÆK HERTIL, RYK FREM HERTIL, HOLD POSITION, SAML HER.

v00.00.09f18 - New Major destinations and obstacle-safe endpoints
- Major orders now create their own visible company path lines and formation destination footprints.
- Selecting the Major shows command-links to all subordinate companies plus their current Major-assigned destinations.
- Major assignment endpoints are checked against Farmhouse/Barn formation footprints and open river positions before use.
- The existing bridge/building route layers still own the legal route TO the selected safe endpoint.

v00.00.09f18 - Reserve/flank decision
- A reserve is NOT mandatory.
- With only one known enemy threat, the Major normally uses the entire battalion front.
- A reserve is considered only for ATTACK/FORSVAR when four active companies exist, at least two enemy units are known, and battalion strength/cohesion are sufficient.
- The freshest/cohesive company is selected when a reserve is justified.
- A flank is attempted only when the known enemy layout leaves one side less occupied and the flank endpoint is legal.
- If the front later becomes heavily threatened, an arrived reserve may be committed toward the threatened company.
- Current enemy knowledge is a temporary prototype radius model (HQ/subordinates approximately 700 m); later LOS/scouting/command-report delay replaces it.

Primary v00.00.09f18 QA
1. Unity compiles without red errors and visible marker reads PROJECT 1864 | v00.00.09f18 TEST.
2. Drag a left-mouse selection rectangle around the Major: the Major becomes selected and the Major panel appears.
3. Right-click ground with Major selected: HQ moves and Major AI becomes OFF.
4. Give one company a manual right-click/H/F/C/Z/X order: that company's AI becomes OFF and it leaves the current Major mission.
5. With that company AI OFF, confirm the existing Major mission does not take it back over.
6. Turn that company AI ON manually: it should rejoin its last Major assignment.
7. Give the Major a NEW FORSVAR HER order: all four company AI states become ON, old manual route/ghost lines disappear, and new Major destination footprints appear.
8. Let the companies reach the defensive line: they must not return to the previous manual destinations afterwards.
9. Place the defensive point near Farmhouse/Barn: no assigned final formation footprint may overlap those hard blockers.
10. Give ANGRIB HER while enemy is within/approaching Long range: companies should stop manoeuvring to engage instead of milling around under fire.
11. With one known enemy, confirm Major normally uses no reserve; with multiple known threats and a healthy battalion, reserve may appear.
12. If a flank is shown, verify it is only chosen toward the less occupied known enemy side and does not land in open water/building footprint.
