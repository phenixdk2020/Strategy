v00.00.09f30a - Command, Square and Officer AI hardening
- Backup baseline before F30A: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f30-pre-f30a-hardening, pointing at the F30 cavalry-core baseline.
- OOB-selected company, Major or Oberstløjtnant selection is preserved through bottom-HUD button presses and point-order completion without forcing a camera move.
- Square presentation hardening removes legacy Line/mission formation boxes for FORMING and active Square, lifts/conforms Square range-sector lines over terrain and guards Square enter/exit so formation changes cannot trigger fake volley smoke.
- Regimental order buttons show a persistent blue pending/active state while subordinate missions still execute that order.
- FORSVAR HER now treats the clicked objective as authoritative. Explicit facing arrows define the defensive front. Without an explicit arrow, AUTO facing uses the nearest relevant enemy and falls back to current formation facing.
- ATTACK HERE company-role allocation now prioritises current men + experience. Assault/front roles receive the strongest available companies; a pure reserve role receives the weakest suitable company.
- AssaultPower QA formula: CurrentStrength * (0.60 + 0.40 * Experience/100). Terrain/bridge safety still has higher priority than role strength.
- Column -> Line pre-contact deployment starts about 35 m outside hostile MaximumRange instead of only 10 m outside it. This gives the physical Line reform time to complete before entering the enemy firing envelope.
- Side Step now handles both close formation overlap and friendly-fire-lane obstruction. If one company blocks another company's live firing lane, the tactically less useful formation steps left/right while preserving Line and facing. If the front company is already productively engaging the same target, the rear company moves to gain a clear lane instead.
- Fire-lane Side Step is AI/higher-command tactical behaviour; a direct manual forced-target order is not silently displaced by this local AI correction.
- Existing charge, melee, Square, bridge route, under-fire reaction and immediate close-contact states remain higher-priority and cannot be overridden by Side Step.
- AI-officer controlled fighting withdrawal remains a committed design rule for a later runtime pass: officers may choose withdrawal based on losses, morale/cohesion, ammunition, flank/rear threat, support and officer stats rather than only player input.

v00.00.09f30 - First cavalry core: Gardehusar + Dragon
- Backup created before F30: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f29z-pre-f30-cavalry-core.
- Adds Assets/Scripts/PrototypeCavalryUnit09F30.cs and PrototypeCavalryManager09F30.cs.
- Adds PrototypeCavalrySquareThreat09F30.cs so a real F30 mounted charge feeds the existing F29 Square threat model. Danish infantry uses the public F29 threat API; Prussian TEST infantry mirrors the same threat score when its Officer AI is ON so Danish cavalry can exercise automatic Square response.
- Spawns one Danish GARDEHUSAR ESKADRON and one DRAGON ESKADRON as TEST cavalry units.
- Both use a shared mounted cavalry core with mounted Line/Column formations, move, hold and charge.
- First QA strengths are 120 Gardehusar and 140 Dragon; these are prototype values, not final historical OOB data.
- Both profiles expose sabre, carbine and pistol capability metadata; weapon-specific firing is intentionally deferred.
- Dragon can SID AF and STIG OP. Dismount leaves horse visuals physically at the dismount point and the dismounted unit must return within 18 m to remount.
- Cavalry movement uses bridge-only crossing when start/goal are on opposite river banks; Column is forced during bridge crossing and the prior formation is restored after crossing.
- Charge contact distinguishes FRONT / FLANK / REAR relative to infantry facing.
- Ready Infantry Square stops the first cavalry charge and causes FALTER; a Square still forming remains vulnerable.
- Charge casualties/shock are first-pass QA values and are not final historical lethality.
- Adds compact TEST cavalry panel at x=310, toggleable with F10. World-click can also select cavalry. FLYT and CHARGE use next battlefield click as point/target.
- No cavalry Officer AI, mounted firearms, morale-break/rout state, horse casualties or OOB integration yet; these are subsequent F30 passes.

v00.00.09f29z - Square face fire + directional smoke
- Four independent 90-degree Square faces with ~25% firepower per side.
- Directional black-powder smoke originates only from the side that actually fires.
- F29Z reads F29V's locked Square heading, so legacy threat-facing cannot rotate the FRONT/RIGHT/REAR/LEFT fire sectors.
- External volley registration keeps ammo/RAMMER feedback correct without fake global fire timers.
