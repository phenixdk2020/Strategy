V10N2 PURPOSE:
- Reduce Game View obstruction from large information/debug boxes.
- Persist player HUD visibility choices between sessions.
- Remove the old rectangular zone-group geometry that visibly extended into the sea and could overlap adjacent groups.
- Clip prototype zone geometry to the same Denmark land scaffold used by the campaign geography layer.
- Keep exact historical 1851 boundaries explicitly separate from the temporary prototype geometry.

HUD / UI:
- New CampaignHudStateV010N2 stores HUD state through PlayerPrefs.
- Technical World Imagery / 3D terrain / basemap / zone-overlay debug panels are hidden by default.
- F1 toggles all non-essential HUD panels.
- F2 toggles the selected zone/city/formation information panel.
- F3 toggles technical/debug panels.
- F4 restores default HUD state.
- INFO and DBG buttons are available in the campaign top bar when screen width allows them.
- Selection panel includes a close button.
- Technical panels no longer intentionally draw on top of each other.
- Top-left build badge and campaign top bar both read CampaignBuildInfo.CurrentVersion.
- GrandCampaignBootstrap.CampaignVersion now derives from CampaignBuildInfo instead of stale v00.00.10m text.

ZONE GEOMETRY:
- GeometryMode=PROTOTYPE_LAND_CLIPPED_GLOBAL_VORONOI.
- The five overlapping geographic bounding rectangles from v10n are removed from zone generation.
- All 20 zone centres now participate in one global nearest-centre partition.
- The global partition is then clipped independently against every land part from GEO_Denmark_NaturalEarth50m.
- Zone polygons therefore stop at the current campaign land-mask/coastline rather than continuing into the surrounding sea.
- Area interiors cannot overlap because every point is assigned by one global nearest-centre partition; shared borders may be rendered by adjacent loops at the same coordinates.
- Boundary material is opaque and slightly thinner to prevent shared lines looking artificially brighter from double alpha blending.
- Overlay remains non-colliding and Z still toggles it.

COASTLINE / HISTORICAL GUARDRAIL:
- The v10n2 coastline clamp uses the current CampaignDenmarkGeography Natural Earth 1:50m land scaffold.
- This is a visual/geographic cleanup, NOT sourced historical 1851 administrative geometry.
- Natural Earth 1:50m is still a simplified coastline and does not guarantee every small fjord, harbour or inlet is represented at World Imagery detail.
- Exact historical zone boundaries remain targeted at DigDag Amt/Region and must later replace the centre-derived geometry while preserving ZoneId and campaign state.

MOVEMENT CLEANUP:
- RMB directly on a city marker now resolves to that city's ZoneId, so city colliders no longer block direct zone march orders.
