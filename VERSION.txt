v00.00.09f9 - Officer engagement-range autonomy retained
- F9 fire policy remains a firing permission / maximum trigger range rather than a mandatory standoff band.
- MEDIUM therefore means the company may open fire out to Medium range; an AI officer may still decide to close into the Close band before engaging.
- Officer preferred engagement range continues to be derived from doctrine, officer tactical skill, aggressiveness and decision noise.
- No forced MEDIUM/CLOSE/LONG standoff override is added in 09f9.

v00.00.09f9 - Higher hit-rate QA
- The prior 1-3 casualties at Close were traced to the existing volley formula: only 58 percent of current strength count as firing men and prototype baseAccuracy was approximately 0.014.
- 09f9 temporarily raises QA base accuracy to 0.050 for Denmark and 0.052 for Prussia.
- The existing continuous distance accuracy curve is retained, so closer fire should still produce substantially more hits than Medium and Long fire.
- Morale remains locked to 100 from 09f8 for this QA pass; cohesion remains active.
- HITTEST-09F8 telemetry remains the primary comparison output for actual distance band and casualties.

v00.00.09f9 - Particle velocity repair
- PrototypeCombatQa09F9 repairs BlackPowderSmoke09F7 Velocity over Lifetime XYZ curves so all three axes use TwoConstants mode.
- X and Z remain zero; Y retains a gentle randomized 0.10-0.45 upward smoke drift.
- This targets the Unity console message: Particle Velocity curves must all be in the same mode.
