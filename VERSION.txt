v00.00.09f12 - Direct ATTACK route authority
- Added PrototypeAttackRouteAuthority09F12 at execution order -5000 so terrain routing is resolved before normal Regiment movement.
- A direct ANGREB target remains the strategic mission target, but forcedTarget tracking is temporarily suspended whenever the legal route requires the river/bridge router or the hard-building router.
- River routing has first priority; approved building avoidance has second priority.
- While a detour is active, Regiment.Update can no longer rewrite destination straight back to the enemy every frame.
- When a clear legal approach exists again, the original forced target is restored automatically and normal attack closing/firing resumes.

v00.00.09f12 - Building waypoint persistence
- PrototypeStaticObstacleRouting09F11 now distinguishes current steering waypoint, already-known final destination and a genuinely new external destination.
- Repeating the same final destination no longer clears the detour queue.
- Farmhouse/Barn detours can therefore progress through corner 1 -> corner 2 -> original final destination instead of repeatedly rebuilding corner 1.
- RequiresDetour() is exposed for the attack-route authority so direct attack orders obey the same approved hard blockers as normal MOVE orders.
