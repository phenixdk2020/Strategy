v00.01.00c5 - QA Lab + Close Soldier Detail
- Builds directly on v00.01.00c4.
- Active OOB remains exactly eight independent Companies: four Danish and four Prussian.
- Actual tactical manpower remains Denmark 759 + Prussia 812 = 1571 men.
- Soldier visual ratio setting from c4 remains available at 1:1, 1:2, 1:3, 1:4, 1:5, 1:10 and 1:20.
- Visual ratio remains presentation-only; tactical manpower, future ammo, casualties, morale and combat state are not scaled by representative count.
- Animated F Line/Column reforming and animated Z/X turning from c4 remain active.
- F8 opens the new QA Lab inspector.
- QA Lab shows selected count, first selected UnitID/name, actual strength, visible representatives, formation, facing, footprint size and reform/turn state.

- F6 runs an automated visual-ratio benchmark over 1:1, 1:2, 1:5, 1:10 and 1:20, measures average frame time/FPS and restores the original ratio afterwards.
- Y toggles selected-Company facing arrows. These are read-only QA overlays showing the actual Company forward direction.
- A new close-range soldier detail overlay adds stronger headgear silhouette, visor/brim, front badge, collar/shoulder detail and a Prussian spike at close range.
- Close soldier detail overlay follows visual ratio and animated Line/Column transitions.
- c5 adds no flags/standards.
- Permanent world labels remain removed; hover info from c4 remains available.
- RMB movement remains OFF and deferred to Gate D.
- Navigation, combat, Officer AI, Brigade AI, artillery and cavalry remain inactive.
