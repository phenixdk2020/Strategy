v00.01.00d2 - Regiment Route + Auto March Deployment + Range Ghost + Standards
- Builds directly on v00.01.00d1.
- Active OOB remains the small QA force: four Danish + four Prussian Companies / 1571 actual men.
- Regiment selection is introduced without adding a competing Regiment movement owner: double-click a Danish Company to select all currently tactical-active Companies belonging to that Regiment.
- Shift + double-click is ready to add another Regiment once more player Regiments are tactical-active.
- Current QA has only one Danish tactical-active Regiment subset, so multi-Regiment player selection cannot be fully exercised until Gate F/I scale-up.
- ALT + RMB click adds route waypoints. Keep ALT held and click several places to construct a route.
- Release ALT, RMB at the final destination, hold/drag RMB to define final facing, then release RMB to confirm.
- Backspace removes the last planned waypoint before confirmation.
- F while planning toggles the FINAL formation Line <-> Column.
- The march itself automatically uses Column regardless of the requested final combat formation.
- Route legs are internally sampled into short approximately 8 m movement segments so contact/deployment decisions can be evaluated between legs while Company remains the sole transform owner.
- When approximately 35 m from the destination, marching Companies deploy back into the chosen final formation before finishing the route.
- If an enemy Company comes within the current QA contact threshold of 160 m while marching in Column, the route is aborted and the Company deploys into Line.
- A public NotifyUnderFire(Company) hook is included for Gate E combat integration. In this QA build, U simulates under-fire for selected Companies so the deployment branch can be tested before live combat exists.
- A cyan ghost route line shows the ordered path.
- Destination ghost footprints remain visible while the order is active.
- The destination ghost includes a facing arrow plus a 60-degree fire-sector cone with three QA range arcs:
  SHORT = 80 m
  MEDIUM = 160 m
  LONG = 260 m
- These D2 range values are QA visualization/tuning values and are NOT historical weapon-source locks yet.
- D2 adds presentation-only standards: one national flag plus one Regiment banner per active Regiment.
- Danish national flag is shown as Dannebrog; Danish Regiment banner uses a provisional dark-red/gold QA design.
- Prussian active Regiment receives a provisional white/black national-style flag and dark Regiment banner.
- Regiment-banner artwork is NOT historical source-locked yet; placement/identity pipeline is what D2 validates.
- Standards follow the average position/facing of their active Regiment Companies and do not own tactical movement.
- Soldier visual ratio settings remain 1:1, 1:2, 1:3, 1:4, 1:5, 1:10 and 1:20 and remain presentation-only.
- D1 articulated soldier renderer/march gait remains the active soldier renderer in D2.
- Hover info remains available through H.
- F10 visual-ratio settings remain available.
- D1 order owner and old C4/C5 drill owners are suppressed in D2 to preserve one command path.
- No obstacle avoidance, bridge/river routing or V3 detour logic yet; those remain the next Gate-D navigation slice.
- Combat, Officer AI and Brigade AI remain inactive.

Controls
- LMB: select Danish Company.
- Double LMB on Danish Company: select all tactical-active Companies of that Regiment.
- Shift + double LMB: add Regiment selection (future-ready for multiple active Regiments).
- Ctrl + double LMB: toggle whole active Regiment selection.
- Shift+LMB: add Company.
- Ctrl+LMB: toggle Company.
- LMB drag: box selection.
- Esc: clear selection / cancel active route planning.
- ALT + RMB click: add route waypoint.
- Keep ALT held + RMB click several places: create multi-point route.
- Backspace: remove last waypoint during route planning.
- RMB at final destination: create final destination ghost.
- Hold RMB + drag: set final facing.
- F during route planning/final ghost: toggle final Line/Column.
- RMB release on final ghost: confirm route/march order.
- U: QA-only simulate UNDER FIRE for selected Companies.
- F10: visual ratio settings.
- H: hover info ON/OFF.
- V: Debug/Clean view.
